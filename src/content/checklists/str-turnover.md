---
title: Short-term rental turnover checklist
service: str_turnover
visibility: public
order: 5
items:
  - Every room reset to the guest-ready standard in your listing photos
  - Bed reset — fresh linens on every bed
  - Towel reset and placement
  - Onsite laundry as the default; offsite available as a premium arrangement
  - Bathroom reset and presentation
  - Kitchen reset, including appliances that guests use
  - Consumable inventory check against your list
  - Floors vacuumed and mopped throughout
  - Visual property check for damage or issues
  - Completion documentation and photos when your workflow needs them
  - Issue report and host notification if something needs your attention
note: Hosts normally supply linens, toiletries, paper goods and consumables; restocking arrangements are a separate agreement.
