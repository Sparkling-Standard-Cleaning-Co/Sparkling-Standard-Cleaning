---
title: Standard residential cleaning checklist
service: standard
visibility: public
order: 1
items:
  - Entryways and common areas dusted, wiped and floors done
  - Kitchen counters, sink, stovetop and appliance exteriors cleaned
  - Cabinet fronts wiped and spot-checked
  - Bathrooms — sinks, vanities, toilets, tubs, showers, mirrors and fixtures
  - Dusting of reachable surfaces, shelves, sills and decor
  - Beds made and rooms tidied
  - Floors vacuumed and mopped, including edges
  - Light switches and door handles wiped
  - Trash emptied and liners replaced
  - Final walkthrough against this checklist
note: This is the normal scope for a maintained home. Inside ovens, refrigerators and cabinets are available as add-ons.
---
