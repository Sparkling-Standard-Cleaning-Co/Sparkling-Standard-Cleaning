---
title: Commercial facility checklist (operational)
service: commercial
visibility: internal
order: 6
items:
  - Scope is defined per facility during walkthrough and recorded before the first visit
  - Entry, common areas, restrooms, work areas and trash per the agreed scope
  - Restroom restocking only when explicitly included in the scope
  - Floors per surface — vacuum, mop, edge attention
  - High-touch points prioritized in shared and customer-facing areas
  - Consistent checklist per facility, reviewed with the manager on request
  - Scheduling confirmed against the facility's operating hours
  - Supplies and equipment responsibility documented in the scope
  - Any issue is reported to the facility contact promptly
note: Internal operational documentation — not published as marketing content.
