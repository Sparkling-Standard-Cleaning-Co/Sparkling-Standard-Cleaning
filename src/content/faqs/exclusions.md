question: Is there anything you don't clean?
answer: Yes, and we're upfront about it. Post-construction cleaning is not a service we offer. We also don't take on specialist or hazardous situations — human waste or biohazard cleanup, severe animal waste, needles or sharps, crime scenes, severe hoarding, mold remediation, serious infestations including bedbugs, hazardous chemical remediation, fire restoration, or work at unsafe heights. These require specialist certification, equipment and insurance that we don't hold, and recommending a specialist is the honest answer. If you're unsure whether your situation fits, just ask — there's no judgment in the question.
