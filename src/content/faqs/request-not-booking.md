question: Does the estimate book my cleaning?
answer: It depends on how you choose to reserve. A pay-later request is not a confirmed appointment; we review it and confirm the date, scope and price with you before scheduling. A paid booking confirms automatically when the payment, address, required photos, requested time and resource availability all verify. If a requirement is outstanding, the booking remains visibly pending while we follow up.
