question: How does commercial quoting work?
answer: Commercial cleaning is quoted after a walkthrough or a detailed scope review — never instantly. You send a walkthrough request with your facility details, we schedule a time to see the space, and you receive a clear scope and quote built around your operating hours and needs. It takes a little more time than a residential estimate, and it's the only honest way to quote a facility.
