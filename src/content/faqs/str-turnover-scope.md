question: What does a rental turnover include?
answer: Cleaning of every room to a guest-ready standard, bed reset with fresh linens, towel reset and placement, onsite laundry as the default, bathroom and kitchen reset to your listing standard, a consumable inventory check against your list, a visual property check, and completion documentation. Hosts normally supply linens, toiletries, paper goods and consumables — restocking arrangements can be added as a separate agreement.
