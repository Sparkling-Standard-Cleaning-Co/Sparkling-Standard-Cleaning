question: How does the instant estimate work?
answer: The online estimate asks a few questions about your home — service type, size, bathrooms, condition, frequency and location — and shows an estimated range. It's built on real labor-hour math, not a guess dressed up as precision. Final scope, condition, access and unusual circumstances can affect the final confirmed price, so every request is reviewed personally before it's confirmed.
