question: How does the instant estimate work?
answer: |
  Our online estimate guides you through five simple steps: When & Where, Service & Home, Condition & Frequency, Your Details, and Extras & Payment. Once completed, you'll receive a cleaning estimate calculated from your service details and estimated labor hours.

  A complete paid booking confirms automatically when the payment, address, required photos, requested time and resource availability all verify. If a requirement is outstanding, the booking remains visibly pending while we follow up. Pay-later requests are reviewed before scheduling.
