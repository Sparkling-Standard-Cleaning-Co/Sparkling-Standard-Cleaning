question: What about an aggressive pet?
answer: If a pet creates a safety risk or won't allow us to clean properly, we'll ask you to secure them during the visit — a closed room, a crate or a visit to a friend's house all work. It's not about fear; it's about keeping your pet, you and your cleaner safe so the cleaning can actually happen. We'll always tell you if it becomes an issue instead of quietly working around it.
