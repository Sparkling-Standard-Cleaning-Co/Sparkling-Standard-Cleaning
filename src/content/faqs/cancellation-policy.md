question: What happens if I need to cancel or reschedule?
answer: We ask for at least 24 hours' notice so the time can be offered to someone else. The cancellation and access terms that apply will be confirmed with your booking. Any fee will be disclosed in writing before it applies, and no cancellation fee is charged unless those terms were provided with your booking. If access is unavailable, we will contact you to work out the next available option.
