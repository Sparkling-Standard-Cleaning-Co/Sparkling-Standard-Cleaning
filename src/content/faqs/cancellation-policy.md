question: What happens if I need to cancel or reschedule?
answer: We ask for at least 24 hours' notice so the time can be offered to someone else. Cancellations with 24 or more hours' notice normally carry no fee. Inside 24 hours — or for same-day cancellations, no-access and lockouts — a partial fee may apply; the exact policy is confirmed with your booking before anything is agreed, and any fee is always discussed openly with you. The founder may exercise reasonable discretion for established clients and genuine emergencies.
