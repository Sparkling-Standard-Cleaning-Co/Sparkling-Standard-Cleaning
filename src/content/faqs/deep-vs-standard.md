question: What's the difference between standard and deep cleaning?
answer: "Standard cleaning covers the full everyday scope: kitchens, bathrooms, floors, dusting, beds and a final walkthrough. Deep cleaning adds the detail work most cleans skip — baseboards wiped by hand, doors and frames detailed, wall spot-cleaning where finishes allow, blinds and sills, and extra attention to buildup throughout. Deep cleaning is also required before every new recurring residential maintenance arrangement begins. If your home has been waiting a while, the estimate will tell you honestly what it needs."
