question: Why is the estimate a range instead of one exact price?
answer: Because a home's real scope isn't fully visible until we're there — condition, buildup, access and unusual requests all change the honest amount of work. The range reflects that reality, and it's why we never present an "instant guaranteed price." Your final price is confirmed personally by the owner before the work begins, and it will never surprise you after the fact.
