  Residential cleaning may be paid securely online through Stripe when Pay Now is offered, or you may submit a request without paying upfront. If a requirement is still outstanding, the booking remains pending and we follow up.

  Commercial cleaning is invoiced monthly with Net 15 terms. No standard deposit or late fee applies initially. Large projects may be considered separately with written terms agreed before work begins.
