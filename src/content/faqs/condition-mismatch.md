question: What if my home's condition is different from what I described?
answer: It happens, and it's handled fairly. If the condition is materially different from the estimate, we may discuss a revised price, adjust the scope to fit the booked amount, or — for larger surprises — pause and schedule a custom quote. Whatever the outcome, it's discussed with you respectfully before work continues. There's no accusation and no surprise bill.
