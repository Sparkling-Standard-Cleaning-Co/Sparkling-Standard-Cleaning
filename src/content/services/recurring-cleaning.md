## Why recurring cleaning gives you the best long-term value

With recurring service from Sparkling Standard, you can enjoy a home that feels fresh, cared for, and ready for everyday life, without letting cleaning take over your schedule.

Whether you choose weekly, biweekly, or monthly service, regular cleanings help prevent buildup, maintain a fresh space, and make every visit more efficient.

Choose the schedule that works best for your home:

- **Weekly Cleaning:** Ideal for busy households that want a consistently clean home and the best value per visit.
- **Biweekly Cleaning:** Our popular middle-ground option for maintaining a tidy, comfortable home with less effort.
- **Monthly Cleaning:** A great fit for smaller spaces or homes that need lighter, ongoing maintenance.

With recurring service from Sparkling Standard, you can spend less time worrying about cleaning and more time enjoying your home. Request a personalized estimate to compare your options and find the schedule that best fits your needs and budget.

## What investing in recurring cleaning with Sparkling Standard looks like

You see the same cleaner each visit. She learns what matters most to you, how you like things arranged, and the perfect process in cleaning your home. In her work you'll see consistency, reliability, and care. Sometimes our founder will bring a trusted helper, but you'll never be serviced by a rotating crew; only familiar faces.
