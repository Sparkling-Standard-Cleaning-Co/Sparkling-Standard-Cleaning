## Why recurring cleaning costs less per visit

This is not a marketing discount — it's simple arithmetic. A home that is cleaned every week or two never has two months of buildup to fight through. The same rooms take less time, so the per-visit price reflects real, honest labor instead of an inflated one-time number with a coupon on top.

Weekly visits get the strongest per-visit value; biweekly is the most popular rhythm for family homes; monthly works for lighter homes and smaller spaces. The estimate shows the difference for your home before you decide.

## The first visit is different — on purpose

We won't pretend a home that hasn't been professionally cleaned recently takes the same time as a maintained one. Your first recurring visit may need extra attention, and if the estimate sees signs of that, it will tell you plainly that an initial detailed clean may be required. After that reset, the home settles into its rhythm.

Occasionally, a home that has been away from professional cleaning for a long time needs a full deep clean first. If that's your home, we'd rather say so up front.

## What staying with us looks like

You'll see the same cleaner each visit. She learns which surfaces matter most to you, how you like things arranged, and the small details your family cares about. If a helper ever joins for a larger job, it's someone trusted — working alongside her, never a random crew.

Recurring customers are the heart of this business. Schedules, preferences and seasonal deep-clean add-ons are all easy to adjust after you're a regular.
