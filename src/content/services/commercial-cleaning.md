## Why commercial cleaning is different

A home has one customer. A facility has a manager, a schedule, a budget and a standard that has to hold up whether the space is empty or full. Commercial cleaning is built on reliability: agreed scope, consistent execution, and communication that doesn't require chasing.

That's why commercial pricing is never instant. The walkthrough exists to see the space honestly — square footage, surfaces, traffic, restrooms, trash, timing constraints — so the quote reflects the actual work instead of a guess that causes problems later.

## A scope you can hold us to

After the walkthrough you receive a clear scope: what's cleaned, how often, when it happens relative to your operating hours, and what it costs. Nothing vague, nothing hidden. Commercial cleaning is invoiced monthly with Net 15 terms; no standard deposit or late fee applies initially. If your needs change — a new space, a busier season, an event — the scope is easy to adjust.

## Scheduling around your operation

Commercial work is scheduled within our published hours, 7:00 a.m. – 7:00 p.m., seven days a week. Churches often prefer weekdays between services; salons often need an early slot before opening; offices often want service before staff arrive. Tell us how your space operates, and the schedule is built around that.
