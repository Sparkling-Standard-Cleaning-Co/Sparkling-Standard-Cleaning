## Built around the walkthrough

A move-out clean is judged by whoever walks the empty home next: a landlord, a property manager, a buyer, a realtor with a clipboard. That's why this service is scoped from the inside out — cabinets emptied and wiped, appliances cleaned inside, the corners and edges that only matter when there is no furniture left to hide them.

The estimate asks about condition honestly. If a home needs more than a standard move-out scope, we'll say so before you book rather than during the walkthrough.

## Timing that respects your deadline

Move-outs run on deadlines. Requests are reviewed personally by Sparkling Standard, and confirmed dates and arrival times are set together so you know exactly when your home will be cleaned. Send the request as early as you can — the sooner we can hold your date, the less stress on moving day.

## Clear scope, no surprises

Your estimate range is followed by a personal confirmation of final scope and price. If anything about the home's condition changes between the estimate and the visit — or the estimate couldn't see everything — we'll talk it through and agree on the right plan before any work starts. If photos help (a few angles of the kitchen and bathrooms are often enough), you'll have a way to share them with your request.
