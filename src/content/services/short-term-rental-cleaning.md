## A turnover product, not "Airbnb cleaning"

Hosts don't need someone to tidy a rental — they need a repeatable system that produces the same guest-ready result every single check-in window. Turnovers are scoped like a production: clean, reset, verify, report.

**Clean.** Every room, guest standard: bathrooms and kitchen reset, floors done edge to edge, surfaces and high-touch points attended to like a guest would notice them.

**Reset.** Beds made with fresh linens, towels placed to your listing's photos, consumables checked against your inventory list so you're never surprised mid-stay.

**Verify.** A completion checklist, photos when your workflow needs them, and a visual property check that catches damage or problems while there's still time to react.

**Report.** Anything that needs a host decision — a broken fixture, a missing item, a stain that needs special treatment — is reported to you promptly, in plain language.

## Built for recurring hosts

Regular hosts don't need to fill out a form from scratch for every turnover. Once you're set up, scheduling settles into a rhythm: recurring dates, known property details, and a process that gets faster and more precise as we learn your listings. Turnover requests go through the same estimate and confirmation flow the first time, then we talk about the repeating arrangement that fits your calendar.

## The honest possibilities

Laundry is planned per property. Onsite laundry is offered where the property equipment and turnover timing permit; offsite laundry is not standard and may be arranged separately. Restocking services can be arranged as a separate agreement. Same-day turnovers are considered case by case when cleaner availability, property access, linens and scheduling are confirmed. There is no automatic same-day guarantee or published premium. Availability and scope are confirmed before the turnover is accepted, so nothing is promised that the schedule cannot support.
