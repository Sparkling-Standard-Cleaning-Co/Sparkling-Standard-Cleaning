## A standard clean, held to a higher standard

Most people know what a rushed clean looks like: streaks left behind, dust moved around instead of removed, and that feeling that something was skipped. We built this company around the opposite idea. A standard house cleaning should reset your home and leave the details you would notice if you looked closely — because we looked first.

That means a real kitchen detail, not a wipe-over. Bathrooms that actually gleam when the light hits the glass. Floors that are mopped to the edge, not around it.

## How a visit works

We start with your estimate and a quick conversation about your home's condition. On cleaning day, we work room by room and finish with a walkthrough of our own checklist. When scheduling allows, you'll get an arrival window rather than a guess — the first appointment of the day can be an exact time.

If anything within the agreed scope doesn't meet your expectations, tell us promptly and we'll work quickly to make it right.

## When a standard clean is the right choice

A standard clean fits a home that is generally maintained and wants consistent attention. If your home hasn't been professionally cleaned in a while — or ever — the first visit may take more time, and the estimate will reflect that honestly instead of promising a price the home can't support.

If you're unsure which service you need, run the estimate: it asks a few quick questions and tells you plainly, including when a deep clean or an initial detailed visit makes more sense.
