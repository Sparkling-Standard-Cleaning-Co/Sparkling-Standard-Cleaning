# Image source register

Internal register for externally sourced imagery. The site's own photography is the goal; until
genuine project photography exists, this register records every licensed stock image in use, its
source, its licence and where it appears. Update it whenever an image is added, replaced or
removed.

**No image in the stock register depicts work performed by Sparkling Standard** All
are representative interiors and process details. Public captions and alt text must never claim
otherwise.

**Genuine owner-supplied imagery (not stock):** `src/assets/images/hayli-founder.webp` — the
official founder portrait of Hayli, supplied by the owner on 2026-10-03 and displayed on
`/about/` and in the homepage founder story with the visible identification "Hayli, Founder &
Owner-Operator". It is genuine owner-provided material (not representative stock, not
AI-generated); it does not appear in the stock table below. Do not reuse or republish it on
unrelated pages, and do not alter the person's appearance.

## Licence

All current images except one are used under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/):
free for commercial use, attribution not required (credited here as good practice), no
standalone resale/redistribution, and no implication that depicted people or properties endorse
this business. No paid licence, subscription or API key is involved.

The single exception is `bathroom-spacious.jpg`, released by its creator under
[Public Domain Mark 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/) — no known
copyright restrictions; credited here as good practice.

## Replacement policy

When the owner supplies genuine photography:

1. Follow `docs/design/IMAGE-GUIDE.md` (format, sizes, privacy checks, permission).
2. Replace the stock file where the image appears and remove its row here.
3. Real work photography takes priority anywhere the current stock image is representative.
4. Keep at least the `proof` collection empty until real, permissioned photos exist.
