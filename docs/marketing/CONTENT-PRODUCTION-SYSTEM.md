# Content production system (proposed)

**Status: PROPOSED — awaiting owner approval.** Nothing in this document is a committed
obligation, a production promise or an approved campaign name. The owner approves the concepts,
titles and schedule before anything is filmed or published, and all production remains
owner-operated with **zero additional spending**.

This is the production layer beneath `docs/marketing/CONTENT-OPERATING-SYSTEM.md` (channel roles,
content pillars) and `docs/marketing/MULTIPLATFORM-OPERATING-PLAN.md` (distribution tiers). It
covers how an idea becomes a published video: concepts, scripts, shot lists, the iPhone 16 Pro Max
+ DaVinci Resolve Studio workflow, publishing instructions and measurement.

## 1. Signature campaign concept — "30 Days. 30 Details." (proposed title)

A recognizable cleaning series showing the details most people never clean, how Hayli cleans them,
what the real result looks like, and the standard behind the business. Each episode names one
detail and proves it on camera.

- **Working title:** "30 Days. 30 Details."
- **Status:** creative proposal. It is **not** an approved commitment to produce 30 consecutive
  daily videos. The operational plan below uses ~4 videos per week (a sustainable owner-operated
  pace), not daily production.
- **Why it fits:** it is the content expression of the brand line *The Details Are Our Standard.*
  and it showcases the business's actual differentiator.
- **Episode anatomy:** one detail → one satisfying demonstration → one visible result → one short
  closing line. Examples of detail categories (never fabricated; only filmed when a real job
  provides them): faucet bases and aerators, switch plates and outlet covers, baseboards and door
  frames, oven door glass, fridge seals and bins, cabinet fronts and handles, bathroom grout
  lines, light-switch grime, window tracks, ceiling-fan blades.

## 2. Content categories

Every video maps to one of these categories:

1. **Founder introduction and journey** — Hayli, her standards, why she started.
2. **Satisfying transformations** — real before/after (permission required).
3. **Overlooked cleaning details** — the signature series.
4. **Practical homeowner education** — maintain-it-between-visits tips.
5. **Authentic behind-the-scenes** — supplies, checklists, the real working day.
6. **Customer transformations and testimonials** — only when genuinely available and authorized.
7. **Local service and availability announcements** — service area, openings, seasons.

Rules: real work only; no staged dirt, no fake before/afters, no invented reviews or results; the
customer's property is filmed only with permission (see `docs/privacy/PHOTO-PRIVACY-SOP.md`); no
identifying information in frame (street numbers, mail, documents, screens, plates, family photos).

## 4. Repeatable video structure

Every short video follows the same production skeleton (~20–45 seconds):

1. **Hook (0–2 s)** — visual motion, an unexpected close-up or the dirty/clean contrast. No long
   intros; the result tease or the problem is the first frame.
2. **Setup (2–5 s)** — one line of spoken or on-screen context ("This is the detail nobody
   cleans").
3. **Demonstration (5–30 s)** — the satisfying process. Real time or gentle speed-ramp; keep the
   cleaning motion visible and satisfying rather than frenetic.
4. **Result (30–40 s)** — hold on the reveal. A wipe or split-screen before/after works best.
5. **CTA (last 2–3 s)** — one line: "Cantonment & Pensacola · estimate in bio" or "Follow for the
   next detail." Never promise pricing, discounts or instant booking.

On-screen text: one idea per frame, large and readable; captions on every video (most viewers
watch muted). Burned-in captions plus a clean export without text for platform-native captions.

## 5. Production workflow — iPhone 16 Pro Max + DaVinci Resolve Studio

### Filming (batch, one session feeds many posts)

- **Shot list first.** Pick 2–4 details from a real job before filming; film each detail from two
  angles (wide context + macro detail) and always capture a clean "before" and an identical
  "after" frame.
- **Format:** 4K or 1080p, 30 or 60 fps, vertical 9:16 for primary channels. When a sequence has
  long-form potential, also capture a 16:9 version. Landscape-only footage is a fallback, not the
  plan.
- **Stability and light:** phone clamp or small tripod for detail shots; use the 2×/5× lens for
  macro work; light the work surface from the side/front, never backlit; lock focus/exposure on
  the detail.
- **Audio:** record Hayli's voice in a quiet room for voiceovers; for on-camera lines, keep the
  phone close or use the phone's directional audio. Music only from platform-provided libraries
  (no copyrighted tracks).
- **Permission:** never film an identifiable interior without the customer's permission; log the
  permission in the job notes the same day. Full rules: `docs/privacy/PHOTO-PRIVACY-SOP.md`.

### Editing (DaVinci Resolve Studio)

- One vertical project per episode: **1080×1920, 30 fps** timeline; import originals from the
  phone; color-match the before/after frames so the reveal is honest.
- Keep a master project per week; cut all of the week's episodes in one editing block.
- Export H.264 MP4 at 1080×1920 for short-form channels; export a 1920×1080 version when a
  platform or embed needs it; archive project files and originals to an external drive.
- Naming convention: `SS_YYYYMMDD_<detail-slug>_<vN>` (example: `SS_20261012_faucet-base_v1`).

### One asset → many publications

| Master asset | Adaptations |
| --- | --- |
| Vertical short (one detail) | Instagram Reel, TikTok, YouTube Short; a still frame becomes a Pinterest pin; a 20-word tip becomes an X/Threads post |
| Longer founder/process cut | YouTube long-form; 60–90 s cut for Facebook and Instagram feed; quote still for Facebook |
| Real photo set (permissioned) | Instagram carousel, Facebook post, Nextdoor-friendly local post, Google Business Profile photo update |

Facebook and Nextdoor should receive locally relevant posts (neighborhood notes, availability,
service explanations) rather than every video reposted mechanically.

## 6. Publishing instructions

- **Primary content channels:** Instagram, TikTok, YouTube. Post the vertical master natively in
  each app; use each platform's own caption/hashtag conventions.
- **Local channels:** Facebook and Nextdoor (both confirmed) get local communication — an
  availability note, a genuine update or a helpful local answer — plus selected videos.
- **Google Business Profile (verified — active, owner-confirmed 2026-10-08):** add real photos,
  accurate service information and periodic updates now; never publish a profile URL or review
  link the owner has not supplied.
- **Tracked links:** use only the inbound links from the generated master
  (`docs/marketing/WHERE-TO-PASTE-UTM-LINKS.md`). Never add UTM parameters to internal website
  navigation, profile links or review links. The live destination must match the link's stated
  purpose (profile vs. estimate).
- **Scheduling:** publish natively; free native scheduling tools are acceptable. Do not add paid
  schedulers or advertising commitments.
- **Captions and accessibility:** burned-in captions for short-form; descriptive alt text where a
  platform supports it; no flashing edits.

## 7. Measurement (what to watch)

Use the reporting hierarchy in `docs/marketing/LEAD-MEASUREMENT-MODEL.md`; the weekly record is
`docs/marketing/WEEKLY-SCORECARD.md`.

| Layer | What to record | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Content views / watch-through | Views, average watch time, saves/shares per video | Each platform's own insights |
| Profile engagement | Profile visits, follower growth, link taps | Platform insights |
| Website visits | Sessions and campaign attribution from tracked links | GA4 (consenting visitors) + UTM data on lead records |
| Inquiries | `cleaning_request_submit`, `commercial_quote_submit`, `str_request_submit` key events, plus the owner inbox | GA4 (owner-confirmed operational) + owner inbox |
| Confirmed bookings | Requests Hayli personally confirmed | Owner records / reservation ledger |
| Completed jobs | Completed work and revenue | Owner records |
| Recurring customers | New weekly/biweekly recurring customers | Owner records / weekly scorecard |

Decision rule: a video is successful when it contributes to **inquiries and bookings**, not when
it accumulates views. Recut the best-performing formats and retire the rest.
