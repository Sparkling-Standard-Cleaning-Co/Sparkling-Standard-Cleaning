# Field content capture — the 10-minute SOP

How to capture marketing assets during a real cleaning job without slowing the work down. This is
the practical layer beneath `CONTENT-ENGINE.md` (what to make) and
`CONTENT-PRODUCTION-SYSTEM.md` (how to edit/publish). Every rule in
`docs/privacy/PHOTO-PRIVACY-SOP.md` applies; if there is any doubt about permission or privacy,
do not capture.

## 0. Before the job

- **Permission first.** Ask at booking for recurring customers; ask on arrival for one-time jobs.
  Log the permission in the job notes the same day (yes/no, date). No permission record → the
  footage does not get published.
- **Pick 2–4 details** for the day from the real job (faucet base, switch plates, oven glass,
  baseboard, window track, grout line, ceiling-fan blade, cabinet handles). Do not stage anything.
- **Check the phone:** battery, storage, clean lens, focus/exposure lock. Filming the details
  you already clean is the whole trick — no extra cleanup sessions.

## 1. The capture set (per detail)

| Step | Shot | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Wide context** | Same angle will be reused for the after; lock the phone position or note it |
| 2 | **Macro before** | 2× or 5× lens; get close; light from the side/front, never backlit |
| 3 | **During clip (10–20 s)** | Film the actual cleaning motion; steady, real-time; one satisfying pass |
| 4 | **Macro after** | Identical angle and light to shot 2 |
| 5 | **Reveal hold (3–5 s)** | Hold still on the result; this is the payoff frame |

For transformations, always capture the same wide angle before and after. For process proof,
capture the method (what product, what motion, in what order) — the honest version, not a
choreographed one.

## 2. Format and settings

- **Video:** 4K or 1080p, 30 or 60 fps, vertical 9:16 for primary channels. Capture a 16:9 version
  only when a sequence has long-form potential (founder/process).
- **Photos:** same phone, HDR on; use the native camera for stills.
- **Stability:** phone clamp or small tripod for macro work; hands are fine for wide shots.
- **Light:** side/front light; avoid harsh backlight and mixed color temperature between before
  and after.
- **Audio:** only matters for founder/voiceover lines; record voice in a quiet room, phone close.
- **Music:** platform-provided libraries only; no copyrighted tracks.

## 3. Privacy scrub (every shot)

Never in frame: street/house numbers, mail or packages, documents, prescriptions, family photos,
screens, alarm panels/keypads, plates, access codes/keys, anything inside personal storage. If
something slips into a frame: delete it — never blur-and-hope. The full never-list and the
revocation rules are in `docs/privacy/PHOTO-PRIVACY-SOP.md`.

## 4. The detail shot checklist (rotate through real work)

Capture only what the job genuinely includes:

- Kitchen: faucet base and aerator, sink rim, switch plates, cabinet fronts/handles, oven door
  glass, fridge seals/bins, backsplash edge, vent hood.
- Bathrooms: faucet base, drain ring, grout lines, mirror edge, toilet base, shower track.
- Living/bedrooms: baseboards, door frames, light switches, ceiling-fan blades, window tracks,
  vents, stair edges.
- Entries: door frames, thresholds, handrails, floor edges.

## 5. Naming, backup and library hygiene

- Naming: `SS_YYYYMMDD_<detail-slug>_<vN>` (example: `SS_20261012_faucet-base_v1`).
- Same-day: transfer originals from the phone to the working drive; archive originals after
  editing (external drive). Phone storage is not a library.
- Library record per published asset: job reference, permission (yes/date), platforms used.
  No permission record → not published.

## 6. Capture time budget

| Job type | Capture time |
| --- | --- |
| Recurring maintenance clean | 5–10 min (1–2 details) |
| First/recurring reset clean | 10–15 min (2–3 details, transformation pair) |
| Deep / move-out clean | 15–20 min (3–4 details, wide before/after) |
| STR turnover | 10 min (reset proof, staged result — host permission) |
| Founder/process piece | Separate session, planned shot list (`VIDEO-DIRECTING-SHEETS.md`) |

If the job runs late, capture one detail well rather than five badly. A single honest clip beats a
rushed batch.

## 7. What never to capture or publish

- No staged dirt, no re-arranged mess, no dark-filter tricks.
- No fake before/afters (including "before" from a different home than the "after").
- No people without explicit written permission (including family members).
- No customer identity, address or private property details.
- No claims attached to a clip the clip cannot prove.

## 8. Quick card (print or save to phone)

1. Permission logged.
2. Wide before.
3. Macro before.
4. During clip (10–20 s).
5. Macro after (same angle).
6. Reveal hold (3–5 s).
7. Scrub: no numbers, mail, documents, screens, plates.
8. Name it `SS_YYYYMMDD_detail_vN`; transfer same day.
