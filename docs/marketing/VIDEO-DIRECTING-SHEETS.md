# Video directing sheets

Ready-to-film sheets for the highest-value videos. They implement the repeatable structure in
`CONTENT-PRODUCTION-SYSTEM.md` §4 (hook → setup → demonstration → result → CTA) and the content
priorities in `CONTENT-ENGINE.md`. Facts spoken on camera must come from owner-approved material
(`src/config/business.ts` founder block) — never invented. Every sheet assumes permission and a
privacy scrub (`FIELD-CONTENT-CAPTURE.md`).

Format for all short-form sheets: vertical 1080×1920, 20–45 s, captions burned in. Music: CapCut
library audio is cleared only for TikTok/CapCut placements; licensed external tracks (for example
Pixabay) are required for Facebook/Instagram/YouTube/website use (`docs/marketing/CAPCUT-OPERATING-STANDARD.md`).

## Sheet 1 — Founder introduction (long + short)

**Objective:** L2 Trust — make Hayli the recognizable face and explain the standard.
**Format:** 60–120 s long cut (YouTube/Facebook/Instagram feed) + 30–45 s short cut (TikTok/Reels/
Shorts).

**Location:** A real, quiet, tidy space (a finished clean or a simple neutral background). Natural
light, phone at eye level, close enough for clean audio.

**Shot list:**

1. Hayli to camera, medium shot — who she is and what she does.
2. B-roll: real detail work from an existing capture set (hands, close-ups).
3. Hayli to camera — why she started (approved background: grew up around her grandmother's
   cleaning business; worked for established local companies; saw rushed production cleaning leave
   the small things undone; studies interior design).
4. Closing line: what "Standards That Make Your Space Sparkle." means to her.

**Suggested lines (adapt, do not add facts):**

- "I'm Hayli, and I started Sparkling Standard because I saw how rushed cleaning leaves the small
  things undone."
- "The toothbrush holder. The fingerprints. The corners nobody thinks about. Those are the job."
- "Every request comes to me. I confirm it, I clean it, and I stand behind it."

**On-screen text:** name + role at first frame; "Standards That Make Your Space Sparkle." at the close.
**CTA:** "Pensacola & Cantonment — estimate link in bio."
**Success measure:** profile visits and tracked-link clicks (not views).

## Sheet 2 — Signature detail demonstration (the repeatable template)

**Objective:** L1 Attention + L3 Proof — the highest-volume format.
**Format:** 20–35 s. One detail per video.

**Shot list (see `FIELD-CONTENT-CAPTURE.md` §1):**

1. Hook (0–2 s): macro of the dirty detail; motion or contrast in frame one.
2. Setup (2–5 s): one line of context ("This is the detail nobody cleans").
3. Demonstration (5–25 s): the real cleaning motion, steady, satisfying.
4. Result (25–32 s): hold on the clean detail; optional split with the before frame.
5. CTA (last 3 s): "Follow for the next detail" or "Cantonment & Pensacola · estimate in bio."

**Suggested hooks:** rotate from `CONTENT-ENGINE.md` §5. Never repeat the previous video's hook.
**On-screen text:** name the detail at the hook ("Faucet base.").
**Success measure:** watch-through and follows; feed the winners into the next capture list.

## Sheet 3 — Transformation reveal

**Objective:** L1 Attention + L3 Proof — the strongest trust asset when genuine.
**Format:** 20–40 s. Requires a permissioned paired job.

**Shot list:**

1. Hook: same-angle before frame with a "before" label; 1–2 s.
2. Process: 2–3 fast but honest clips of the actual work.
3. Reveal: same-angle after frame; hold.
4. Optional split-screen: before | after, same light, no filters.
5. CTA: "First clean or recurring — link in bio."

**Rules:** same angle, same light, same home; never stage dirt; never use one home's "before" with
another's "after."
**Success measure:** saves/shares + inquiries; this format is also the best door-knocking proof
(see `ACQUISITION-CHANNELS.md` §4).

## Sheet 4 — First clean vs. maintained (recurring value)

**Objective:** L4 Decision + L6 Retention — sells the recurring model.
**Format:** 25–40 s. Requires a real recurring home (permissioned) or an honest explanation.

**Shot list:**

1. Hook: "This home gets cleaned every two weeks. Watch what that means."
2. Contrast: a maintained surface (quick pass) vs. the effort a first clean takes (time-lapse or
   honest clips from a first clean).
3. Explanation: what stays easier — kitchen, bathrooms, floors, the "reset" feel.
4. CTA: "Ask about weekly or biweekly — estimate link in bio."

**Facts:** frequencies (weekly/biweekly/monthly) are verified in the pricing model; never quote a
price on camera — the estimator and personal confirmation handle that.
**Success measure:** recurring requests (the primary business objective).

## Sheet 5 — Move-out detail checklist

**Objective:** L4 Decision — move-out/move-in season demand.
**Format:** 30–45 s.

**Shot list:**

1. Hook: "The deposit walkthrough comes down to these details."
2. Fast cuts of checklist items (oven glass, inside fridge, baseboards, window tracks, bathroom
   grout, cabinet fronts).
3. One "what landlords actually check" line.
4. CTA: move-out page link (tracked).

**Rules:** never promise a deposit outcome; describe what is cleaned, not what a landlord will
decide.
**Success measure:** move-out page visits and inquiries.

## Sheet 6 — STR turnover walkthrough

**Objective:** L2 Trust + L4 Decision — host acquisition.
**Format:** 30–60 s. Host permission required; never show guest belongings or identifying details.

**Shot list:**

1. Hook: "Checkout at 11, check-in at 3. Here's the turnover."
2. Timed sequence: reset of kitchen, bathrooms, linens, staging check.
3. One line on the host's real pain (review risk, same-day turns).
4. CTA: STR host outreach link (tracked).

**Rules:** no guest information, no lockbox codes, no address; staging shots only of the finished
result.
**Success measure:** STR inquiries; reuse in host outreach (`STR-HOST-OUTREACH.md`).

---

## Sheet 7 — "Ask Hayli" answer

**Objective:** L2 Trust + L4 Decision — answer a real question.
**Format:** 20–40 s. One genuine comment/question per video.

**Shot list:**

1. Hook: the question on screen ("Someone asked: how often should you really clean?").
2. Hayli answers honestly and briefly (two or three sentences, no sales pitch).
3. CTA: "Comment your question."

**Rules:** answer truthfully even when the answer is "it depends"; never turn the answer into a
discount or a promise.
**Success measure:** comments, follows, profile visits.

---

## Sheet 8 — Neighborhood note (local, often non-video)

**Objective:** L5 Local — density and recognition.
**Format:** Photo carousel or 15–25 s clip; also a text post for Facebook/Nextdoor.

**Shot list / content:**

1. Honest local note: "We're cleaning in [real neighborhood] this week" with a permissioned detail
   shot (never a street number).
2. One line about route availability ("now scheduling in the neighborhood").
3. CTA: local tracked link (Facebook/Nextdoor-specific UTM).

**Rules:** only real served areas (`src/config/business.ts` service area); never publish a customer
address; never imply a discount.
**Success measure:** local link clicks and door-knocking recognition (neighbors mention seeing the
post).

## Batch shoot day (one session feeds the week)

1. Pick 3–4 details from the day's real job; log permission.
2. Capture the full set per detail (`FIELD-CONTENT-CAPTURE.md` §1).
3. Capture one founder line (optional) in a quiet moment.
4. Transfer and name everything the same day (`SS_YYYYMMDD_detail_vN`).
5. Edit in one CapCut Desktop block: one vertical project per episode, 1080×1920, 30 fps
   (`CONTENT-PRODUCTION-SYSTEM.md` §5, `CAPCUT-OPERATING-STANDARD.md`).

## Do and do not

| Do | Do not |
| --- | --- |
| Film the real work, real time, real result | Stage dirt, retouch results or fake a before/after |
| Keep hooks honest and specific | Promise prices, discounts or instant booking |
| Burn in captions (most viewers watch muted) | Use copyrighted music |
| Capture wide + macro pairs | Film identifiable interiors without permission |
| Keep the founder's facts owner-approved | Add years, certifications or client counts |
| One idea per video | Stack five messages into 30 seconds |
