# Commercial outreach playbook

Commercial accounts are a core business objective. One strong owning page
(`/commercial-cleaning/`), a dedicated church page, a walkthrough-first process, and honest
scoping. No fabricated references, no instant commercial pricing.

## Segments (priority order)

| Segment | Why it fits | Entry point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Churches | Owner's desired vertical; consistent needs; evenings/weekdays | Church outreach card + packet |
| Small offices / professional suites | Recurring, after-hours friendly | Commercial packet |
| Property managers | Common areas + turnovers; portfolio leverage | Property-manager packet |
| Apartment communities | Common areas, model units, turnovers | Apartment packet |
| Salons / barbershops | After-close cleaning; detail culture fit | Commercial packet |
| Gyms / fitness | High-touch surfaces; reliable schedule | Commercial packet |
| Medical / dental office common areas | Detail standards; daytime after-hours | Commercial packet |
| Retail | Floors, glass, restrooms | Commercial packet |
| Small warehouses | Periodic detail; scope-defined | Commercial packet |

## Process

```
identify → contact → walkthrough → scope → quote → follow-up → recurring service
```

1. **Identify** — 10 targets per week from a physical drive or map walk (independent
   businesses only, no franchise cold-blast).
2. **Contact** — in person is fastest: leave the packet, get a name and the best contact. Log it.
3. **Walkthrough** — use the commercial form (`/commercial-cleaning/#walkthrough`) as the
   intake standard: facility type, size, frequency, timing, current situation. Always see the
   space before quoting.
4. **Scope** — a written scope: what's cleaned, how often, when, and what it costs. Vague scopes
   create disputes. Use `docs/operations/SERVICE-SCOPE-MATRIX.md`.
5. **Quote** — custom, honest, built from labor-hours and walking pace, not guesses
   (`docs/operations/ESTIMATOR-CALIBRATION.md`).
6. **Follow-up** — one polite follow-up at ~5 days, one at ~2 weeks, then quarterly nurture.
7. **Recurring** — the goal is always a recurring agreement with a review checkpoint at 30 days.

## Materials

- Packets use the generated QRs from `public/marketing/qr/` — every QR is attributed
  (`commercial-packet`, `property-manager-packet`, `church-outreach`).
- One-page scope + what-to-expect sheet; reference the site for depth rather than printing
  everything.
- Never print "references available" until real references exist and consent is given.

## Estimating commercial work (quick anchors)

- Time the walkthrough: measure restrooms, floors by surface, glass, and high-touch density.
- Frequency changes labor materially — a weekly office is faster per visit than a monthly one.
- After-hours expectations adjust price (access, alarms, lone working).
- Church cleaning plans should respect service schedules and volunteer teams
  (`/church-cleaning/` explains this publicly).

## Do-not list

- No post-construction, no biohazards, no restoration, no specialist certification work —
  decline professionally and refer specialists where possible.
- No instant commercial numbers, ever. The site's commercial form never promises pricing.
