# STR host outreach playbook

Short-term rental turnovers are a flagship service. Hosts buy **reliability**, not "cleaning" —
their reviews, their income and their calendar depend on every turnover being right.

## What hosts care about (lead with these)

1. **Reliability** — showing up, on time, every turnover, including same-day flips.
2. **Consistency** — the same guest-ready standard every time, not a different result each stay.
3. **Proof** — completion checklist, photos when the workflow needs them, issue reports.
4. **Communication** — problems reported while there's still time to react.
5. **Repeat scheduling** — a rhythm, not a form per turnover.

## Process

```
identify → contact → property details → first turnover (evaluate) → recurring arrangement
```

1. **Identify** — Airbnb/VRBO listings in the service area with turnover gaps in reviews
   ("cleanliness" mentions), local host Facebook groups, property managers with STR portfolios.
2. **Contact** — hosts respond best to specific, low-friction messages: what you handle
   (linens, consumables check, photo proof), what you don't (restocking is a separate
   arrangement), and one clear ask (a walkthrough or a trial turnover).
3. **Property details** — use the STR form on `/short-term-rental-cleaning/#turnover-request`
   (beds, baths, check-out/in times, laundry, same-day flips, proof needs).
4. **First turnover** — over-deliver: checklist, photo set, an issue report even if minor.
5. **Recurring arrangement** — agreed rhythm, stored property notes, direct scheduling channel.

## Materials

- STR host packet with the `airbnb-host-packet` QR (attributed).
- Sample completion checklist + sample photo report (sanitized — real proof only, privacy
  checked).
- The site page `/short-term-rental-cleaning/` explains the product; link it directly.

## Pricing posture

- Turnovers are labor-based: beds + baths + size + laundry + same-day constraints.
- Same-day turnovers, offsite laundry, restocking and consumable purchasing are premium or
  separate agreements — never bundled for free. The agreement must stay profitable.
- The instant estimate gives a range for standard turnovers; unusual properties go through
  manual confirmation.

## Do-not list

- Do not promise 24/7 emergency service (not approved).
- Do not claim host referrals or partnerships that don't exist.
- Do not photograph guest belongings, documents or identifying details — ever
  (`docs/privacy/PHOTO-PRIVACY-SOP.md`).
