## 3. Scope

The standard move-in/move-out scope may include:

- standard residential cleaning under Section 2.4 of the Core Terms;
- inside cabinets, drawers and closets when selected;
- inside refrigerator and oven when selected;
- interior windows and reachable tracks when selected;
- detailed baseboards, door frames and reachable trim when selected;
- removal of light moving dust and debris; and
- other tasks listed in the signed schedule.

**Excluded:** construction dust and debris, paint, drywall repair, hazardous
materials, biohazards, mold remediation, carpet extraction, pressure washing,
garage or patio work, and restoration or repair.

**3.1** A post-construction or renovation clean requires a separate written
quote and may be declined.

**3.2** If the property is not vacant or utilities are not active, we may
reschedule or adjust the scope.

## 4. Condition and final walkthrough

**4.1** The estimate assumes normal moving dust and ordinary condition. Heavy
buildup, pet waste, smoke residue, hoarding or hazardous conditions may require
a revised quote or a specialist service.

**4.2** Where a landlord, buyer, seller or property manager requires a
walkthrough, the responsible party must be available or provide a written
acceptance process. We do not guarantee a third party's acceptance standard.

**4.3** You or your representative should inspect the work promptly. Missed
items within the agreed scope will be corrected under Section 12 of the Core
Terms.
