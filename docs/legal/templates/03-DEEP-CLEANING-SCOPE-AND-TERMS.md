## 1. Purpose

Deep Cleaning is a one-time, intensive baseline clean for a home that has not
been professionally maintained recently, has heavy buildup, or is starting a new
recurring arrangement. It is not a restoration, construction cleanup or
biohazard service.

## 2. Included scope

Subject to safe access and the condition of the property, Deep Cleaning may
include:

- all standard residential cleaning tasks in Section 2.4 of the Core Terms;
- detailed dusting of reachable surfaces, ledges, sills and door frames;
- hand-wiped baseboards and reachable trim;
- detailed bathroom cleaning, including tile and grout surfaces that are sound
  and not damaged;
- detailed kitchen cleaning, including appliance exteriors, cabinet fronts and
  reachable backsplash surfaces;
- interior windows and reachable tracks when selected;
- extra attention to buildup on reachable surfaces;
- light organization and tidying as agreed; and
- other tasks listed in the signed service schedule.

## 3. Limits and exclusions

**3.1** Deep Cleaning does not include and we will not perform:

- biohazard, human or animal waste, needle or sharps cleanup;
- mold remediation, fire restoration or hazardous chemical remediation;
- severe hoarding, pest infestation or structurally unsafe conditions;
- carpet or upholstery extraction, pressure washing, garage or patio work unless
  separately quoted;
- paint removal, restoration, repair, caulking, grout replacement or surface
  refinishing;
- high or ladder work that cannot be performed safely; or
- removal of permanent stains, etching, hard-water damage or pre-existing damage.

**3.2** We do not promise that every stain, discoloration or odor will be
removed. We protect finishes and follow manufacturer instructions.

## 4. Condition and time

**4.1** Deep Cleaning takes substantially longer than a maintenance clean. The
scheduled duration is an estimate based on the information provided.

**4.2** If the actual condition requires materially more work, we will stop and
discuss options before continuing. Options may include a revised written quote,
a reduced scope for the current visit, rescheduling, or a recommendation for a
specialist service.

**4.3** We will not charge more than the amount you approved.

## 6. Required first visit for recurring service

When Deep Cleaning is the required first visit of a new recurring arrangement,
it is priced separately from the ongoing per-visit price. The ongoing price
begins with the next visit and never changes a completed or paid Deep Cleaning
invoice.

## 7. Safety

You will secure pets, valuables, firearms, medications and fragile items. You
will tell us about any hazardous condition before work begins. We may stop work
for unsafe conditions and document why.
