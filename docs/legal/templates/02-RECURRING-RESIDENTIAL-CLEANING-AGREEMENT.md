## 1. Agreement and term

**1.1** This Residential Recurring Cleaning Agreement is between **Sparkling
Standard LLC**, doing business as **Sparkling Standard Cleaning Co.**, and the
customer named in the signature block.

signing, you confirm that you received and reviewed both documents.

**1.3** The arrangement begins on the confirmed first-visit date and continues
until either party terminates it under Section 14 of the Core Terms, or until the
schedule is changed in writing.

**1.4** A new recurring residential arrangement requires a **Deep Cleaning
first, without exception**, so the ongoing rhythm starts from a known baseline.

**4.1** The ongoing price is the price for each recurring visit after the first
Deep Cleaning, subject to the scope and condition assumptions in this agreement.

**4.2** If the property condition, occupancy, pets, square footage or requested
scope changes materially, we will discuss a revised written price before the
change takes effect.

**4.3** Payment for each residential visit is due immediately upon completion
unless prepaid or an approved written arrangement says otherwise.

**4.4** A promotion applies only under its published terms. FRIENDS15, when
assigned to the account, is 15% off the eligible initial cleaning and 15% off
eligible recurring cleaning, has no visit limit and no automatic expiration while
assigned, does not stack with Community Support, and never changes a historical
paid invoice.

**4.5** Recurring autopay is **not active** unless the owner provides a separate
signed Recurring Payment Authorization. No saved card is charged after service
without that separate authorization.

## 5. Scheduling and changes

**5.1** The preferred day and arrival window are targets. We will tell you about
a necessary change as soon as practicable.

**5.2** You may request a skip or reschedule under the Cancellation and
Rescheduling Terms. Repeated changes may make the preferred slot unavailable.

**5.3** We may adjust the schedule for holidays, severe weather, unsafe
conditions or staffing. We will offer the nearest reasonable alternative.

## 6. Quality and communication

**6.1** Report a missed item promptly under Section 12 of the Core Terms so we can
inspect and correct it.

**6.2** We will document material condition changes and any approved scope change.

## 7. Termination

Either party may terminate the recurring arrangement with reasonable written
notice. Amounts due for services already performed remain payable. The Florida
statutory cancellation right applies as described in Section 11.2 of the Core
Terms.
