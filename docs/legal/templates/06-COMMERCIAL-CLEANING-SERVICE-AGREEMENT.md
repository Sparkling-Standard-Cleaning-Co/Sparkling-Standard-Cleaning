## 3. Scope and service standard

**3.1** The signed facility schedule defines the tasks, areas and frequency. Work
not listed is not included.

**3.2** The service standard is a professional, consistent clean appropriate for
the facility type and the agreed schedule. It is not a restoration, construction
cleanup, infection-control service, or a guarantee of a particular inspection
score.

**3.3** Restroom, breakroom, high-touch surface, floor care and trash tasks are
performed only as listed in the schedule.

**3.4** Consumable supplies, paper products, soap and liners are supplied by the
client unless the schedule says otherwise.

**3.5** Specialty work, periodic floor care, carpet extraction, window work above
reach, pressure washing and post-construction cleanup require a separate written
scope and quote.

## 4. Facility access and security

**4.1** The client will provide lawful access instructions, alarm codes, key or
badge procedures, parking information and any background-check or site-induction
requirements before the first service.

**4.2** We will not bypass a security system, force entry, or allow unauthorized
persons into the facility. Access credentials are used only for the agreed
service.

**4.3** The client will secure cash, drugs, controlled substances, weapons,
records, valuables and confidential information. We do not open locked drawers,
safes or restricted rooms.

**4.4** If a site requires an escort, the client will provide the escort at the
scheduled time. A delayed escort may reduce the completed scope or require
rescheduling.

## 5. Client responsibilities

**5.1** The client will provide accurate facility information, safe working
conditions, functioning utilities, adequate lighting, safe access and a
designated area for equipment and supplies.

**5.2** The client will disclose known hazards, including biohazards, sharps,
chemical hazards, mold, pest infestation, structural issues and restricted
areas.

**5.3** The client will remove or secure confidential documents, personal
information, cash, controlled substances, firearms and valuables from areas to be
cleaned.

**5.4** The client will not ask our team to perform work outside the agreed scope
or outside our insurance or competence.

## 6. Changes, quality and reporting

**6.1** A scope or schedule change requires written confirmation and may change
the price.

**6.2** The client will report a service concern promptly and allow a reasonable
opportunity to inspect and correct it. We will document the review and any
corrective work.

**6.3** We may provide service reports, checklists or photographs for quality and
dispute records. Client-specific reporting requirements must be stated in the
schedule.

**7.3** The client will pay an undisputed invoice by the due date. A disputed
amount must be identified in writing promptly so it can be reviewed.

**7.4** [OWNER DECISION / LEGAL REVIEW] No standard late fee or interest charge is
approved today. A late charge may apply only if disclosed in a written fee
schedule and permitted by law.

## 8. Term and termination

**8.1** The initial term and renewal terms are stated in the schedule. If no term
is stated, the arrangement is month-to-month.

**8.2** Either party may terminate for convenience with the written notice stated
in the schedule, or 30 days if none is stated.

**8.3** Either party may terminate immediately for a material breach that is not
cured within a reasonable written cure period, for unsafe conditions, or for
nonpayment of an undisputed invoice.

**8.4** Amounts due for services already performed remain payable.
