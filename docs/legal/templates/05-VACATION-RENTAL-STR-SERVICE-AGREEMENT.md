## 3. Turnover scope

A standard turnover may include:

- removing guest trash and replacing liners;
- cleaning bathrooms, kitchens, counters and appliance exteriors;
- making beds and resetting linens when linen handling is selected;
- vacuuming and mopping floors;
- dusting reachable surfaces;
- spot-cleaning mirrors, glass and reachable surfaces;
- reporting lost and found, damage, missing supplies or safety concerns; and
- other tasks listed in the signed schedule.

**Excluded:** carpet extraction, upholstery cleaning, exterior windows above
reach, pressure washing, pest control, mold remediation, biohazards, repairs,
maintenance, landscaping, pool service, guest property storage and shipping.

**3.1** We are not responsible for guest belongings. Lost and found items will
be reported and handled as you direct, subject to reasonable storage limits.

**3.2** We do not guarantee a guest review, rating or platform acceptance.

## 4. Damage, security and privacy

**4.1** You will tell us about security cameras, alarms, noise devices and any
restricted areas. We will not disable or tamper with a security system.

**4.2** We will report visible damage, missing items, safety hazards and
unauthorized conditions. We are not responsible for guest-caused damage or
missing property.

**4.3** Guest and owner privacy is handled under Section 8 of the Core Terms and
the Privacy Policy. Do not ask us to access personal guest accounts, devices or
documents.

## 5. Linen and supplies

**5.1** Linen and supply responsibilities are stated in the schedule. If we
launder linen, the schedule must state the quantity, turnaround expectations and
any additional charge.

**5.2** We are not responsible for linen quality, stains or wear that existed
before our handling, or for an insufficient owner-supplied quantity.

**5.3** Missing or depleted supplies will be reported. Restocking is performed
only when selected and priced or reimbursed by written agreement.
