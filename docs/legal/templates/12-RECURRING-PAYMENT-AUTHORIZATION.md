## 1. Scope

This authorization would allow Sparkling Standard Cleaning Co. to charge the
customer's saved payment method automatically for recurring residential cleaning
after each completed visit, only under the terms below.

It does not authorize a charge today, does not authorize a tip, and does not
authorize any amount the customer has not approved.

## 3. Charge rules

**3.1** A charge is made only after the visit is completed and the final amount
is determined under the signed service agreement and any approved add-on
authorization.

**3.2** The charge will not exceed the authorized per-visit maximum unless the
customer approves a higher amount in writing for that visit.

**3.3** No charge is made for a visit that was canceled, skipped or not performed.

**3.4** A promotion, FRIENDS15 benefit or gift certificate is applied under its
own terms before the card charge. A gift certificate remains payment tender, not
a discount.

**3.5** A receipt is provided for each charge.

## 5. Canceling or changing the authorization

**5.1** The customer may cancel this authorization at any time by written notice
or through the customer account controls made available by the owner. Cancellation
takes effect before the next charge after we have a reasonable opportunity to
process it.

**5.2** The customer may change the maximum amount, payment method or frequency
by written notice.

**5.3** Canceling automatic payment does not cancel the cleaning arrangement or
amounts already due for completed services.

## 6. Failed payments

If a charge fails, we will notify the customer and may request another payment
method. We will not repeatedly retry a failed charge in a way that violates
processor rules or applicable law. No late fee applies unless separately
disclosed and permitted by law.

## 7. Records and consent

We retain this authorization, the consent version, the signer identity, the
timestamp and the document hash. The customer may request a copy.
