## 1. Requesting a change

**1.1** To cancel or reschedule, contact Sparkling Standard Cleaning Co. using
the phone, text, email or contact form listed on the website. Keep the
confirmation we send.

**1.2** We ask for at least **24 hours' notice** so the time can be offered to
another customer. A shorter-notice request will be handled as availability
allows.

**1.3** A requested change is not effective until we confirm it in writing or by
a clear electronic confirmation.

## 2. Statutory cancellation right

**2.1** For a contract for future consumer services provided on a continuing
basis, Florida law gives the customer the right to cancel without penalty or
obligation within **three business days** after entering the contract, and to a
full refund of payments made.

**2.2** The customer may also cancel if a doctor orders that the customer cannot
physically receive the services, or if the services cease to be offered as stated.

**2.3** If the customer cancels for either statutory reason, we may keep only the
pro rata portion of the price for services already used or completed, plus the
cost of related goods the customer consumed or retained.

**2.4** A valid statutory cancellation refund will be issued within 20 days after
we receive notice. This right is in addition to any other right the customer has.

## 3. Fees

**3.1** No standard cancellation fee currently applies.

**3.2** If the owner later approves a cancellation or late-rescheduling fee, it
must be disclosed in a clearly identified written fee schedule before the
customer accepts it, limited to a reasonable estimate of documented business
loss, and it will not be enforced during the statutory three-business-day
cancellation period.

**3.3** If a customer cancels after we have traveled to the property or begun
setting up, we may discuss reimbursement of documented, reasonable travel and
preparation costs. No such charge is automatically collected by the website.

## 4. Failed access and lockouts

**4.1** If access fails because of incorrect access information, a changed code,
an armed alarm, an inaccessible lockbox or an unreachable contact, the visit may
be treated as a failed appointment.

**4.2** We will attempt to contact the customer and document the visit. Any
disclosed failed-access fee must be in the written fee schedule. No automatic fee
is currently collected. [OWNER DECISION]

## 5. Late arrival and weather

**5.1** If we must change an appointment because of weather, unsafe conditions,
staffing or an earlier job running long, we will contact the customer and offer
the nearest reasonable alternative.

**5.2** A delayed arrival is not a cancellation by the customer. We will not
charge a cancellation fee for a delay we caused.

## 6. Recurring arrangements

**6.1** A recurring customer may request a skip or reschedule under this exhibit.
Repeated short-notice changes may make the preferred slot unavailable.

**6.2** Terminating a recurring arrangement does not cancel amounts due for
services already performed.

## 7. Refunds

**7.1** An eligible refund is returned to the original payment method where
possible, or by another lawful method agreed in writing.

**7.2** A gift-certificate-funded payment is restored to the certificate or
handled as the gift terms require. Gift certificates are payment tender, not a
discount.

## 8. Electronic acceptance

By signing or accepting the applicable service agreement, the customer confirms
receipt of this exhibit.
