## 3. Scope

**3.1** The signed schedule defines the areas, tasks and frequency. Work not
listed is not included.

**3.2** Typical scope may include sanctuary seating areas, lobbies, classrooms,
nurseries, offices, restrooms, breakrooms and reachable floors and surfaces.

**3.3** We do not clean or move religious items, sacramental items, candles,
altars, musical instruments, electronics, cash, offering records or confidential
documents unless the schedule specifically authorizes the task and the item is
safe to handle.

**3.4** We do not perform restoration, mold remediation, biohazard cleanup,
post-construction cleaning, pest control or repair.

## 4. Facility responsibilities

**4.1** The church will secure cash, offering records, confidential member
information, religious items, electronics and valuables before service.

**4.2** The church will disclose known hazards, restricted areas, alarm
procedures, security requirements and any volunteer or staff expectations.

**4.3** The church will provide functioning utilities, safe access and adequate
lighting.

**4.4** The church will not ask our team to perform work outside the agreed scope
or outside our insurance or competence.

**5.1** An eligible church cleaning may receive the owner-approved **10%

deduction** for either party.

**5.2** The church discount applies only to an eligible invoice and never changes
a historical paid invoice.

**5.3** Church and facility cleaning is due **Net 15 calendar days from invoice
issuance**, unless an approved written arrangement overrides it.

**5.4** A tax exemption is honored only after the church provides a valid,
current exemption certificate acceptable to the owner. We do not invent an
exemption.

## 6. Term and termination

The initial term and renewal terms are stated in the schedule. If no term is
stated, the arrangement is month-to-month. Either party may terminate with the
written notice stated in the schedule, or 30 days if none is stated. Amounts due
for completed services remain payable.

## 7. Insurance and licensing

owner has supplied verified details and counsel has approved the disclosure. A
