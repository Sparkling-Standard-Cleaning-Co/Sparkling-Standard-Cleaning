## 6. Emergency and access failure

**6.1** If access fails, we will attempt to contact you using the number provided
and document the visit. We may wait a reasonable time, then leave.

**6.2** If an emergency requires us to leave the property or contact emergency
services, we will do so and notify you as soon as practicable.

**6.3** We are not responsible for a delay or missed service caused by incorrect
access information, a changed code, a failed lockbox, an armed alarm or an
unreachable contact.

## 7. Authorization and revocation

**7.1** You may revoke this authorization at any time by written notice. Revocation
takes effect when we receive it and have a reasonable opportunity to update the
service record.

**7.2** This authorization ends when the service period ends, when the service
agreement ends, or when you revoke it.

**7.3** This authorization does not authorize charges, saved-card payments or
marketing use of photographs.
