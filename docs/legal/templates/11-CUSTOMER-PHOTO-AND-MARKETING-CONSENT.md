## 1. Purpose

Sparkling Standard Cleaning Co. may take condition or before-and-after
photographs for quality control, dispute resolution and internal records under
Section 8 of the Core Terms. This separate consent is only about **public
marketing use**.

## 2. What the customer is consenting to

By selecting "Yes" below, the customer gives Sparkling Standard Cleaning Co.
permission to use selected photographs of the cleaned areas for marketing,
including:

- the Sparkling Standard website;
- Google Business Profile and other social platforms;
- printed or digital brochures and advertisements; and
- before-and-after presentations.

## 3. Limits that protect the customer

**3.1** No photograph will be published if it contains:

- a street number, house number or mailbox name;
- mail, documents, screens, prescriptions, financial information or personal
  data;
- family photographs, identification, keys, alarm codes or security details;
- people's faces or identifying features without separate written permission;
- the inside of a home beyond the cleaned area; or
- anything the owner or customer reasonably considers private.

**3.2** Photographs will be cropped or excluded if needed to protect privacy.

**3.3** No customer name, address, email, phone number or testimonial will be
published with a photograph without separate written permission.

**3.4** The customer may withdraw this consent at any time by written notice.
We will stop using the photographs for future marketing within a reasonable time.
Withdrawal does not require removing already-printed material that is no longer
under our control, but we will remove the photographs from our website and
active social posts as soon as practicable.

**3.5** This consent does not transfer ownership of the customer's property or
give away any privacy right beyond the limited marketing use described here.

## 4. No payment or endorsement

This consent does not require the customer to write a review, give a testimonial,
refer another customer, or make any statement. No payment is promised for the
consent.
