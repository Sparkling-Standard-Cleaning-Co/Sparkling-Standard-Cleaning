## 1. Parties and authority

**1.1** This agreement is between **Sparkling Standard LLC**, doing business as
**Sparkling Standard Cleaning Co.** ("Sparkling Standard," "we," "us," "our"),
and the customer identified in the signed service schedule ("you").

**1.2** You confirm that you are the property owner, the owner's authorized
agent, a tenant authorized to arrange cleaning, or a property manager authorized
to bind the owner. If you arrange service for a property you do not own, you
confirm that you have authority to grant access and approve the scope and
charges.

**1.3** A signed service schedule, written proposal, written change order or
approved add-on authorization becomes part of this agreement. If those documents
conflict, the most specific signed document controls, then this agreement, then
the published Terms of Service.

**1.4** No employee or representative may orally waive a term unless the waiver
is confirmed in writing by an authorized owner.

## 2. Service scope

**2.1** We perform only the cleaning described in the signed service schedule.
Anything not listed is not included.

**2.2** Optional services are performed only when selected in writing and
priced. Custom work requires a separate written quote. Excluded work is not
performed under this agreement.

**2.3** We do not promise removal of every stain, mark or odor. We protect
finishes and manufacturer instructions first.

**2.4** Standard residential cleaning generally includes reachable counters and
sinks, appliance exteriors, bathrooms (sinks, toilets, tubs, showers, mirrors),
dusting of reachable surfaces, vacuuming and mopping of floors including edges,
beds made, light tidying, switches and door handles, and trash removal with new
liners.

**2.5** Optional residential work may include inside cabinets, drawers or
closets; inside refrigerator or oven; interior windows; exterior ground-reachable
windows; one load of laundry; bed linen change; dishes; general organization; and
pet-hair intensive work, only when selected and priced.

**2.6** Carpet cleaning, upholstery cleaning, pressure washing, garage work and
patio work are custom-quote services.

**2.7** Hand-wiped baseboards, full door and frame detailing, wall spot-cleaning,
blinds and sills are not included unless the signed schedule says otherwise.

## 3. Excluded and hazardous work

**3.1** We do not perform post-construction cleaning; biohazard or human waste
cleanup; severe animal waste; needle or sharps cleanup; crime-scene cleanup;
severe hoarding; mold remediation; serious infestations including bedbugs;
hazardous chemical remediation; fire restoration; unsafe height or ladder work;
or any work requiring specialist certification, equipment or insurance we do not
hold.

**3.2** If a condition requiring specialist work is discovered, we will stop and
tell you. We will not attempt work outside our competence or insurance.

**3.3** If conditions are unsafe, threatening, infested, structurally unsound or
outside the agreed scope, we may stop work immediately and document why.

## 4. Scheduling and arrival

**4.1** A submitted request, estimate or temporary hold is not a confirmed
appointment. An appointment is confirmed only when we confirm the scope, date and
price with you, or when a complete paid booking passes every server verification.

**4.2** The first appointment of the day may be scheduled for an exact arrival
time. Later appointments use an arrival window to account for travel and the
honest duration of earlier work. A requested time is a preference until
confirmed.

**4.3** Published service hours are 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m., seven days per week,
Central Time, unless a written schedule says otherwise.

**4.4** A home or facility takes as long as it honestly takes. We do not rush
work to protect a clock. If the agreed scope cannot be completed in the scheduled
window, we will tell you and agree on the next step before leaving.

**4.5** You agree to provide accurate day-of-service contact information and to
respond to access questions within a reasonable time. Repeated unresponsiveness
may require rescheduling.

## 5. Access, keys, alarms and security

**5.1** We do not retain customer physical keys. Access uses an authorized
temporary method, a customer-controlled lockbox, or an access method documented
in the Customer Service Access Authorization.

**5.2** You will provide working entry instructions, gate or building codes,
parking information, alarm instructions and any building requirements before the
appointment, and you will keep that information current.

**5.3** If an alarm is armed, you will disarm it, provide a temporary code, or
tell us not to enter. We will not bypass a security system, force a door, or
remain in a property where we are not authorized to be.

**5.4** If access is unavailable, if the authorized contact cannot be reached
when presence is required, or if access is withdrawn after we arrive, we will
attempt to contact you and document the visit. [OWNER DECISION: any disclosed
failed-access fee. No automatic failed-access fee is currently collected.]

## 6. Customer responsibilities and valuables

**6.1** You will secure cash, jewelry, firearms, medications, financial
documents, heirlooms and other valuables before service.

**6.2** You will tell us before work begins about fragile, antique, improperly
secured, damaged or unusually delicate items and surfaces.

**6.3** You will provide accurate information about square footage, room counts,
condition, buildup, pets, access, photos, existing damage and specialty
conditions. The estimate depends on the accuracy of that information.

**6.4** You will secure aggressive or uncontrolled animals before the visit.
Pets are welcome when they are safe and do not prevent the work from being done
properly.

**6.5** Harassment, threats, discrimination or unsafe conduct toward our team
ends the appointment.

## 7. Condition, scope changes and reassessment

**7.1** If actual conditions differ materially from the information provided, we
may need to reassess before work begins.

**7.2** An on-site reassessment is a documented review. It is not a punishment
and not automatically a fee. After reassessment we may confirm the original scope
and price; offer a revised written quote; adjust the scope to fit the agreed
amount; reschedule; or decline unsafe, hazardous or specialist work.

**7.3** We will not charge more than the amount you approved. If you decline a
necessary revised scope before work begins, either party may decline to proceed.

**7.4** A change order or Additional Service/Add-On Authorization may be recorded
in writing, including email or text, before the additional work is performed. If
you authorize additional work orally during a visit, we will confirm the added
scope and price in writing as soon as practicable.

## 8. Photographs, privacy and marketing

**8.1** Photos you upload are private and are used only to prepare and confirm
your estimate and cleaning. They are never posted publicly. Upload only the areas
being cleaned, and never identification documents, screens, mail or personal
paperwork.

**8.2** We may take before-and-after or condition photographs for quality
control, dispute resolution and internal records. We will not publish
identifiable photographs of your home or belongings for marketing without your
separate written permission in the Customer Photo and Marketing Consent.

**8.3** Photographs are stored in private storage with access limited to
authorized personnel and are retained only as long as needed for the purposes
above or as required by law. You may ask us to delete non-essential photographs,
subject to records we must keep for tax, insurance or dispute purposes.

**8.4** Our handling of personal information is described in the published
Privacy Policy. If this agreement and the Privacy Policy conflict about privacy,
the Privacy Policy controls for privacy matters.

## 9. Price, taxes, payment timing and invoices

**9.1** The online estimate is a proposed estimate, not a guaranteed final price.
Final scope, condition, access and unique circumstances can affect the confirmed
price.

**9.2** Residential cleaning is due immediately upon service completion unless
prepaid. Commercial and church cleaning is due Net 15 calendar days from invoice
issuance unless an approved customer-specific written arrangement overrides it.

**9.3** Payment may be made through the methods we publish or agree in writing.
We do not store card data in the application. Payment processing is performed by
Stripe or another disclosed processor.

**9.4** If you believe a charge is wrong, contact us first so we can investigate.
We will not retaliate against a customer for exercising a legitimate cardholder
dispute right.

**9.5** Overpayments and duplicate payments are refunded or credited after
verification. Do not send payment to an account or link that did not come from an
authorized Sparkling Standard communication.

**9.6** [OWNER DECISION / LEGAL REVIEW] No standard late fee or interest charge is
approved today. If the owner adopts one, it must be disclosed in a written fee
schedule, accepted before it applies, and reviewed for compliance with Florida
and Alabama law.

## 10. Promotions and account benefits

**10.1** A promotion is valid only under its published terms, eligibility,
service restrictions, deadlines and redemption limits.

**10.2** At most one cleaning promotion applies to a quote unless the promotion
is expressly marked as stackable with Community Support. The better eligible
offer wins when offers cannot stack.

**10.3** **FRIENDS15** is an owner-controlled customer benefit: 15% off an
eligible initial cleaning and 15% off eligible recurring cleaning, with no visit
limit and no automatic expiration while it remains assigned to the account. It
does not stack with Community Support. It may apply to eligible previously
scheduled unpaid or uncompleted appointments. It never changes a historical paid
invoice. The owner may remove it.

**10.4** Community Support is a self-attested eligibility discount. It never
requires sensitive records and never stacks with FRIENDS15.

**10.5** Gift certificates are payment tender, not a discount. They are applied
after promotion and tax rules and never reduce the certificate's face value.

**10.6** We will not alter a historical paid invoice to add a later promotion.

## 11. Cancellation, rescheduling and failed access

**11.1** We ask for at least 24 hours' notice so the time can be offered to
someone else. To cancel or reschedule, contact us using the phone, text, email or
contact form listed on our website and keep the confirmation we send.

**11.2 [STATUTORY RIGHT]** For a contract for future consumer services provided
on a continuing basis, Florida law gives you the right to cancel without penalty
or obligation within three business days after you enter the contract, and to a
full refund of payments made. You may also cancel if a doctor orders that you
cannot physically receive the services, or if the services cease to be offered as
stated. If you cancel for either of those reasons, we may keep only the pro rata
portion of the price for services already used or completed, plus the cost of
related goods you consumed or retained. Refunds for a valid cancellation will be
issued within 20 days after we receive notice. This right is in addition to any
other right you have.

**11.3** No standard cancellation fee currently applies. If the owner later
approves a cancellation or late-rescheduling fee, it will be disclosed in
writing, in a clearly identified fee schedule, before you accept it, and limited
to a reasonable estimate of documented business loss. It will not be enforced
during the statutory three-business-day cancellation period.

**11.4** Repeated cancellations, repeated access failures or abusive scheduling
behavior may lead to declining future bookings.

**11.5** If you cancel after we have traveled to the property or begun setting
up, we may discuss reimbursement of documented, reasonable travel and preparation
costs. No such charge is automatically collected by this website. [OWNER
DECISION]

## 12. Satisfaction, complaints and corrections

**12.1** If something within the agreed cleaning scope was missed, report it
promptly. We will review it and provide reasonable corrective cleaning at no
additional charge for verified missed work. This is not an unconditional refund
promise and does not extend to unrelated work, restoration, repair or conditions
outside the scope.

**12.2** Please allow us a reasonable opportunity to inspect and correct the work
before arranging a third-party remedy. We will respond in writing where
appropriate.

**12.3** A complaint about damage is handled under Section 13. A complaint about
cleaning quality is handled under this section.

## 13. Damage, liability and insurance

**13.1** We work carefully and treat every home with respect. You are responsible
for telling us about fragile, antique, improperly secured, damaged or unusually
delicate items and surfaces before work begins, and for securing valuables.

**13.2** We are responsible for damage caused by our own negligent work within
the agreed scope. We are not responsible for pre-existing damage, ordinary wear,
improperly installed fixtures, damage caused by defective products you supplied,
or conditions we could not reasonably see or access. This is not a waiver of
liability for our own negligence.

**13.3** If you believe we damaged something, tell us promptly and preserve the
item and any photographs. We will investigate, document the claim and work toward
a fair resolution. Nothing in this agreement limits a right that cannot legally
be limited.

**13.4 [NO INVENTED CLAIMS]** We make no public claim about insurance coverage
amounts, bonding or licensing unless the owner has supplied verified details and
counsel has approved the disclosure. The owner's commercial insurance policy is a
private business record.

**13.5** Each party will cooperate in good faith with an insurance or damage
investigation, including providing photographs, receipts where available, and a
written description of the loss.

## 14. Termination

**14.1** Either party may terminate a recurring arrangement with reasonable
written notice. You remain responsible for services already performed and
approved charges.

**14.2** We may suspend or terminate service immediately for unsafe conditions,
harassment, threats, repeated access failures, nonpayment of an undisputed
invoice, or a request to perform excluded or unlawful work.

**14.3** Termination does not erase record-retention obligations or rights that
survive by law.

## 15. Electronic records and signatures

**15.1** By signing electronically, you consent to use electronic records and
signatures for this agreement and its schedules under the Florida Electronic
Signature Act and the federal E-SIGN Act, and to receive disclosures
electronically. You may request a paper copy by contacting us.

**15.2** Your typed legal name is your signature. We record the signer name,
email, consent version, timestamp, document version, document hash and audit
events. The signed copy and its audit record are retained privately.

**15.3** A signed document is not the same as a payment authorization. A separate
Recurring Payment Authorization is required before any saved card may be charged
after service.

## 16. Governing law and disputes

**16.1** Please contact us first so we have a chance to resolve the issue. We will
review the facts, respond in writing where appropriate, and try to reach a fair
resolution.

**16.2** This agreement is governed by Florida law. Any dispute that cannot be
resolved may be brought in a court of competent jurisdiction serving Escambia
County, Florida, except where a consumer protection law gives you a non-waivable
right to another forum.

**16.3** These terms do not create an arbitration agreement, a class-action
waiver, or a waiver of any right that cannot lawfully be waived.
