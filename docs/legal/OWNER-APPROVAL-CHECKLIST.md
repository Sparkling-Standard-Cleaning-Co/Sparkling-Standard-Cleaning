# Owner Approval Checklist - Core Agreements

**Version:** 2026-11-19.v1  
**Status:** All items unchecked. Nothing is approved or in effect.

Use this checklist for the four agreements the business will use most. The
editable Markdown originals are in `docs/legal/templates/`; branded PDFs are
generated with `npm run legal:render` and copied to the owner review folder.

## 1. Core Cleaning Service Terms (SS-CORE-2026-11)

Review the shared clauses used by every customer agreement.

- [ ] Parties and authority are correct (Sparkling Standard LLC d/b/a Sparkling Standard Cleaning Co.).
- [ ] Service scope, optional work, exclusions and hazardous-work limits match the real service.
- [ ] Scheduling and arrival windows match published operations (7:00 a.m.-7:00 p.m., seven days).
- [ ] Access, key, alarm and valuables rules match the actual process (no retained physical keys).
- [ ] Photo/privacy language matches the private photo process and marketing consent.
- [ ] Payment timing is correct: residential immediately on completion unless prepaid; commercial/church Net 15.
- [ ] Promotions section matches FRIENDS15, Community Support nonstacking and gift-certificate tender rules.
- [ ] Florida three-business-day cancellation language is correct.
- [ ] No standard late/cancellation fee is implied.
- [ ] Damage/liability language is reasonable and does not waive non-waivable rights.
- [ ] No insurance, bonding or licensing claim is made without verified details.
- [ ] Electronic-signature consent and paper-copy procedure are approved by counsel.
- [ ] Governing law, venue, notices and contact details are correct.

**Counsel should specifically confirm:** the cancellation/refund clause, the
limitation-of-liability language, the e-signature evidence package, and whether
Alabama service needs separate venue/consumer wording.

## 2. Residential One-Time Cleaning Agreement (SS-CUST-RES-ONE)

- [ ] The Core Terms are attached and referenced.
- [ ] Standard scope table matches the approved residential checklist.
- [ ] Optional and custom-quote items are clearly separated.
- [ ] Price, promotion and gift-tender table is accurate.
- [ ] Access Authorization and Cancellation Terms are attached when applicable.
- [ ] Photo/marketing consent is separate and optional.
- [ ] Signature block and service-address confirmation are usable.

**Counsel review:** scope/exclusion wording, damage reporting window, and
whether the Core Terms are incorporated effectively.

## 3. Residential Recurring Cleaning Agreement (SS-CUST-RES-REC)

- [ ] First visit is stated as a required Deep Cleaning.
- [ ] First-visit price and ongoing per-visit price are separate.
- [ ] Frequency, arrival window, crew size and duration are clear.
- [ ] FRIENDS15 language matches the owner policy: 15% initial and recurring, no visit limit, no automatic expiration while assigned, nonstacking with Community Support, no historical invoice changes.
- [ ] Autopay is clearly **not active** unless a separate signed authorization exists.
- [ ] Termination and schedule-change rules match the actual process.
- [ ] Grandfathered arrangements are not repriced by the agreement.

**Counsel review:** recurring-service cancellation rights, price-change notice,
and the interaction between the first Deep Cleaning and the ongoing schedule.

## 4. Deep Cleaning Scope and Terms (SS-CUST-DEEP)

- [ ] Included tasks match the approved deep-cleaning checklist.
- [ ] Limits and exclusions are accurate (no restoration, biohazard, mold, post-construction or unsafe work).
- [ ] No guarantee of stain/odor removal is made.
- [ ] Condition reassessment and revised-quote process is clear.
- [ ] Price and first-visit recurring language is accurate.
- [ ] Safety stop and specialist-referral language is acceptable.

**Counsel review:** the scope/exclusion list and the reassessment/price-change
clause.
