# Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning Agreement

**Document ID:** LEG-6C749FA3
**Version:** 2026-11-22.remediation.v1
**Status:** Owner review draft — not effective until approved
**Category:** Customer - Residential

## 1. Parties and Incorporation

1.1 This Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning Agreement is between Sparkling Standard LLC, doing business as Sparkling Standard Cleaning Co., and {{customer.legal_name}} for the property at {{property.address}}.

1.2 This Agreement incorporates the Core Cleaning Service Terms (the "Core Terms"). The Core Terms control general rights and responsibilities. This service-specific Agreement controls the obligations unique to this service, and the signed service schedule controls the property, scope, date, price, discount, tax, and payment timing. If a provision conflicts, the order of precedence in Section 2 of the Core Terms applies.

## 2. Required Property Condition

2.1 Move-in and move-out cleaning is performed on an empty-home basis. The property must be vacant, or the remaining contents must be disclosed and agreed in writing before the visit.

2.2 The customer will remove personal belongings, furniture, construction debris, and hazardous items before the visit. Sparkling Standard is not a moving, hauling, or debris-removal service.

2.3 If the property is not empty or the condition differs materially, Sparkling Standard may reassess the scope and price under Section 14 of the Core Terms.

## 3. Approved Scope

3.1 The approved move-in/move-out checklist is:

- Every room cleaned top to bottom to an empty-home standard.
- Inside cabinets, drawers, and closets.
- Inside the refrigerator and oven.
- Baseboards, door frames, and doors detailed.
- Bathrooms detailed from shower corners to behind fixtures.
- Window sills and interior glass reachable from the floor.
- Floors vacuumed and mopped throughout, including edges.
- Light fixtures and switch plates wiped.
- Final empty-home walkthrough against this checklist.
3.2 Optional work, including interior windows above floor reach, exterior windows, garage, patio, carpet extraction, and appliance detail beyond the listed scope, requires a separate written quote.

## 4. Utilities and Water

4.1 The customer will ensure that water and electricity are available and that the property can be safely accessed.

4.2 If utilities are unavailable, Sparkling Standard may reschedule the visit. A repeated utility failure caused by the customer may be treated as a failed-access event.

## 5. Access and Key Handoff

5.1 The customer will provide access under the Residential Access Authorization. Sparkling Standard does not retain residential physical house keys.

5.2 If a real estate agent, property manager, or settlement representative controls access, the customer will identify that person and confirm the access method in writing.

5.3 A key handoff must be documented. Sparkling Standard will not accept an unidentified key or an unsecured key left on the property.

## 6. Remaining Belongings and Obstructions

6.1 The customer will identify any item that must remain in the property and any area that must not be cleaned.

6.2 Sparkling Standard will not move or dispose of remaining belongings unless the signed schedule expressly authorizes it.

6.3 An obstruction that prevents cleaning of a listed area will be documented. The area may be excluded or the visit may be rescheduled.

## 7. Exclusions and Restoration Limits

7.1 Sparkling Standard does not perform construction cleanup, paint removal, caulking, grout replacement, carpet extraction, upholstery extraction, pressure washing, mold remediation, pest control, biohazard cleanup, or repair.

7.2 Sparkling Standard does not promise removal of permanent stains, odors, etching, hard-water damage, or pre-existing damage.

7.3 The customer will identify fragile or damaged surfaces before work begins.

## 8. Final Inspection and Documentation

8.1 Sparkling Standard performs a final walkthrough against the approved checklist and may photograph the completed areas for quality and dispute records.

8.2 The customer should report a missed item promptly. Verified missed work within the agreed scope will be corrected under Section 20 of the Core Terms.

8.3 A damage claim is handled under Section 21 of the Core Terms.

## 9. Price, Payment, Cancellation, Insurance, and Electronic Signature

9.1 The signed schedule states the price, selected optional work, discount, tax, and total. Payment is due upon completion unless prepaid.

9.2 The cancellation, rescheduling, and failed-access terms in Section 19 of the Core Terms apply.

9.3 Sparkling Standard Cleaning Co. maintains commercial general liability insurance with limits of $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 general aggregate. Evidence of coverage is available upon request. Coverage is subject to the terms, conditions, exclusions, and limits of the applicable insurance policy. The existence of insurance does not expand or reduce either party's contractual rights or responsibilities.

9.4 The electronic signature and records terms in Section 24 of the Core Terms apply.

## 10. Execution

10.1 The customer and Sparkling Standard execute this Agreement and the signed move schedule.
