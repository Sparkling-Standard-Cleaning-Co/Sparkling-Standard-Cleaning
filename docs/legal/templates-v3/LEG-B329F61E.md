# Vacation Rental / STR Turnover Agreement

**Document ID:** LEG-B329F61E
**Version:** 2026-11-22.remediation.v1
**Status:** Owner review draft — not effective until approved
**Category:** Customer - Short-Term Rental

## 1. Parties and Incorporation

1.1 This Vacation Rental / STR Turnover Agreement is between Sparkling Standard LLC, doing business as Sparkling Standard Cleaning Co., and {{customer.legal_name}} for the short-term rental property at {{property.address}}.

1.2 This Agreement incorporates the Core Cleaning Service Terms (the "Core Terms"). The Core Terms control general rights and responsibilities. This service-specific Agreement controls the obligations unique to this service, and the signed service schedule controls the property, scope, date, price, discount, tax, and payment timing. If a provision conflicts, the order of precedence in Section 2 of the Core Terms applies.

1.3 The customer confirms authority to arrange turnover service for the property and to approve the host workflow, access method, and charges in the signed schedule.

## 2. Turnover Schedule and Guest Departures

2.1 The signed schedule states the turnover dates or recurring turnover pattern, the guest departure time, the check-in time, and the arrival window.

2.2 Sparkling Standard needs a workable window between guest departure and check-in. If the window is too short, the scope must be reduced or the turnover rescheduled.

2.3 The customer will tell Sparkling Standard promptly about an early check-in, late checkout, same-day booking, or other change that affects the turnover window.

2.4 A turnover is not a Deep Cleaning. A property that requires deep work, restoration, or extraordinary condition correction is handled under Section 10.

## 3. Approved Turnover Scope

3.1 The approved turnover checklist is:

- Every room reset to the guest-ready standard in the listing photographs.
- Bed reset with fresh linens on every bed.
- Towel reset and placement.
- Laundry plan confirmed per property: onsite where available, offsite by arrangement.
- Bathroom reset and presentation.
- Kitchen reset, including appliances that guests use.
- Consumable inventory check against the host list.
- Floors vacuumed and mopped throughout.
- Visual property check for damage or issues.
- Completion documentation and photographs when the host workflow requires them.
- Issue report and host notification when something needs attention.
3.2 The signed schedule identifies any selected optional work, including interior windows, exterior ground-reachable windows, organization, and inventory restocking.

## 4. Laundry and Linens

4.1 The customer will state whether laundry is performed onsite, offsite, or by a separate laundry provider, and will provide an adequate supply of linens and towels.

4.2 The signed schedule states whether linen replacement, stain treatment, or offsite laundry is included and how the cost is handled.

4.3 Sparkling Standard is not responsible for a linen shortage, a damaged linen supplied by the customer, or a delay caused by an unavailable laundry facility.

4.4 A linen or supply shortage will be documented and reported to the host. Replacement is not purchased without the host's authorization.

## 5. Consumables and Restocking

5.1 The customer will provide a current inventory list and state which consumables Sparkling Standard should check, restock, or report.

5.2 Restocking is included only when the signed schedule says so and the supplies are available at the property or are purchased under an approved arrangement.

5.3 Sparkling Standard will not purchase a replacement item, furniture, or appliance without the host's written authorization.

5.4 Missing or low supplies are reported to the host. A guest-caused shortage is not a cleaning defect.

## 6. Host-Supplied Inventory and Property Instructions

6.1 The customer will provide current property instructions, including access, alarm, parking, trash, laundry, pet, pool, and building requirements.

6.2 The customer will identify any locked owner closet, restricted room, or item that must not be moved.

6.3 The customer will keep the listing standard and photographs current. A material change to the listing standard may require a scope review.

## 7. Damage Observations and Missing Supplies

7.1 Sparkling Standard performs a visual property check and reports visible damage, missing supplies, or unsafe conditions to the host.

7.2 A visual check is not a property inspection, engineering review, or inventory audit. Sparkling Standard does not certify the condition of the property or the completeness of the host's inventory.

7.3 The host is responsible for guest communication, security deposits, and any claim against a guest.

7.4 Sparkling Standard will document a observed issue with a photograph or written note when the host workflow requires it.

## 8. Access Problems

8.1 The customer will provide a working access method under the Residential Access Authorization or a separate property-specific instruction. Sparkling Standard does not retain residential physical house keys.

8.2 If access fails, Sparkling Standard will attempt to contact the host and document the visit. A failed turnover caused by unavailable access may be treated under Section 19 of the Core Terms.

8.3 A lockbox or temporary code should be changed when access ends or when a guest or vendor should no longer have access.

## 9. Guest-Caused Extraordinary Conditions

9.1 A turnover assumes a customary guest departure condition. Excessive trash, biohazard, smoking residue, pest activity, unauthorized pets, severe staining, or property damage may require work outside the standard turnover.

9.2 Sparkling Standard may stop, document the condition, and request supplemental authorization before performing extraordinary work.

9.3 Sparkling Standard will not perform biohazard, needle, sharps, crime-scene, mold, pest, or restoration work.

## 10. Supplemental Authorization and Pricing

10.1 The signed schedule states the standard turnover price, selected optional work, discount, tax, and total.

10.2 Supplemental work requested by the host before or during the turnover requires the host's written authorization and a stated price. Sparkling Standard will not charge an open-ended supplemental amount.

10.3 A guest-caused condition is reported to the host. Sparkling Standard does not charge the guest directly.

10.4 Payment is due under the signed schedule or invoice terms. The Core Terms govern late payment and disputes.

## 11. Completion Reporting

11.1 Sparkling Standard reports completion and any issue through the method agreed with the host.

11.2 Completion photographs are taken only as part of the approved workflow and are handled under Section 15 of the Core Terms. They are not published without separate marketing consent.

11.3 The host should report a missed item promptly. Verified missed work within the agreed scope is corrected under Section 20 of the Core Terms.

## 12. Insurance and Electronic Signature

12.1 Sparkling Standard Cleaning Co. maintains commercial general liability insurance with limits of $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 general aggregate. Evidence of coverage is available upon request. Coverage is subject to the terms, conditions, exclusions, and limits of the applicable insurance policy. The existence of insurance does not expand or reduce either party's contractual rights or responsibilities.

12.2 The electronic signature and records terms in Section 24 of the Core Terms apply.

## 13. Execution

13.1 The customer and Sparkling Standard execute this Agreement and the signed turnover schedule.
