# Saved Payment Method Authorization (After-Service Charges)

**Document ID:** LEG-55C03080
**Version:** 2026-11-22.remediation.v1
**Status:** Owner review draft — not effective until approved
**Category:** Customer - Payment Authorization

## 1. Purpose and Distinction

1.1 This Saved Payment Method Authorization applies to a one-time after-service charge for a completed service. It does not authorize a recurring charge.

1.2 A recurring arrangement requires the separate Recurring Payment Authorization.

1.3 The customer may pay by another published method instead of signing this authorization.

## 2. Authorization

2.1 The signed authorization states the customer, property, covered service, maximum charge, and expiration.

2.2 The customer authorizes Sparkling Standard to charge the saved method only after the covered service is completed and the final invoice is available.

2.3 Sparkling Standard will not charge more than the maximum or for a service outside the authorization.

2.4 A charge above the maximum requires a new signed authorization.

## 3. Invoice and Notification

3.1 Sparkling Standard will provide the final invoice or receipt showing the amount charged and the service.

3.2 The customer will receive notice before the charge when the signed authorization requires notice.

3.3 A charge is not made merely because an earlier invoice is overdue.

## 4. Revocation and Expiration

4.1 The customer may revoke this authorization in writing before the charge.

4.2 The authorization expires on the date stated in the signed schedule or, if no date is stated, after the covered service and a reasonable billing period.

4.3 Revocation does not affect a charge already earned for completed work.

## 5. Failure and Disputes

5.1 A declined method is not retried indefinitely. Sparkling Standard will contact the customer for another method.

5.2 A disputed amount will be reviewed under Section 18 of the Core Terms. A verified error will be corrected.

## 6. Records and Security

6.1 Sparkling Standard retains the signed authorization, version, timestamp, invoice, and receipt. Full card numbers and security codes are not stored.

6.2 Payment processing is performed by Stripe or another disclosed processor.

## 7. Execution

7.1 The customer and Sparkling Standard execute this authorization. The customer confirms that the covered service, maximum, and expiration are accurately stated.
