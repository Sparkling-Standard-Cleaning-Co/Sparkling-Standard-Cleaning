# Electronic Signature and Records Addendum

**Document ID:** LEG-0E4847B5
**Version:** 2026-11-22.remediation.v1
**Status:** Owner review draft — not effective until approved
**Category:** Customer - Electronic Signature

## 1. Consent to Electronic Records

1.1 By affirmatively consenting and typing a legal name in the approved signing session, the signer agrees to use electronic records and electronic signatures for the documents presented and to receive related disclosures electronically.

1.2 The signer confirms that the signer can access and review the documents, can print or save a copy, and has a device and software that support the signing page.

1.3 The signer may request a paper copy of a signed document by contacting Sparkling Standard.

## 2. Scope

2.1 This consent covers the service agreement, service schedule, access authorization, cancellation terms, payment authorization, photo consent, and any addendum presented in the signing session.

2.2 This consent does not cover a government form that requires a separate process, including Form I-9.

2.3 This consent does not authorize a charge. A separate payment authorization is required.

## 3. Hardware and Software

3.1 The signing page requires a current web browser, internet access, and the ability to view and retain a PDF or text record.

3.2 If the signer cannot access the electronic record, the signer should contact Sparkling Standard for another approved method.

## 4. Paper Copies

4.1 The signer may request a paper copy of a signed document. Sparkling Standard may provide a PDF copy when a paper copy is not reasonably required.

4.2 A paper copy does not change the signed version or the electronic record.

## 5. Withdrawal

5.1 The signer may withdraw consent to future electronic records by written notice to admin@sparkling-standard.com.

5.2 Withdrawal does not affect a record already signed or a pending obligation to complete a signature by another approved method.

5.3 Sparkling Standard will confirm the withdrawal and identify the effect on pending documents.

## 6. Contact Information

6.1 The signer will keep the email address and telephone number used for signing current.

6.2 A notice sent to the last recorded contact is effective unless the signer has provided a valid replacement.

## 7. Attribution and Evidence

7.1 The signer's typed name is the signature.

7.2 Sparkling Standard records the signer name, email, consent version, exact document version and SHA-256 hash, timestamp, signing token or session evidence, and signed content.

7.3 The executed record identifies each document signed and the signature status.

7.4 A signature is attributable to the signer when the signing session is bound to the signer's email and the recorded evidence is consistent.

## 8. Delivery and Retention

8.1 The executed copy is made available through the signing page or customer portal.

8.2 Sparkling Standard retains the signed record and audit evidence privately for the applicable retention period.

8.3 The signer may download or print the executed copy.

## 9. No Overstatement

9.1 Sparkling Standard will not describe the signing system as providing a capability it does not implement.

9.2 Form I-9 remains under its separate compliant manual workflow.

## 10. Execution

10.1 The signer and Sparkling Standard execute this Addendum as part of the signing session.
