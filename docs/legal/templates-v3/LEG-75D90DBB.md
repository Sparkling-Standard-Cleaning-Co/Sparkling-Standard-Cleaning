# Additional Service and Add-On Authorization

**Document ID:** LEG-75D90DBB
**Version:** 2026-11-22.remediation.v1
**Status:** Owner review draft — not effective until approved
**Category:** Customer - Additional Work

## 1. Authorization

1.1 The customer authorizes only the additional services listed in the signed add-on schedule, at the stated price, for the stated property and visit.

1.2 The add-on schedule identifies the service, quantity, price, and any condition or limitation.

## 2. No Implied Scope

2.1 No additional service is included by implication, verbal request, or the presence of the team.

2.2 An add-on does not include repair, restoration, or work outside its description.

2.3 An add-on does not extend a fixed appointment beyond the agreed window unless the schedule says so.

## 3. Payment and Cancellation

3.1 The add-on is subject to the approved payment, cancellation, and access terms.

3.2 The add-on price is due with the service invoice unless prepaid.

3.3 A cancellation or failed-access amount is governed by Section 19 of the Core Terms.

## 4. Execution

4.1 The customer and Sparkling Standard execute this authorization. The signed schedule is incorporated into the service agreement.
