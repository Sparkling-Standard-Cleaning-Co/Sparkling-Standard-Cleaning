# Residential Recurring Cleaning Agreement

## 1. Parties and Incorporation

1.1 This Residential Recurring Cleaning Agreement is between Sparkling Standard LLC, doing business as Sparkling Standard Cleaning Co. ("Sparkling Standard"), and {{customer.legal_name}} (the "customer") for the property at {{property.address}}.

1.2 This Agreement incorporates the Core Cleaning Service Terms (the "Core Terms"). The Core Terms control general rights and responsibilities. This service-specific Agreement controls the obligations unique to this service, and the signed service schedule controls the property, scope, date, price, discount, tax, and payment timing. If a provision conflicts, the order of precedence in Section 2 of the Core Terms applies.

## 2. Initial Deep Cleaning Is Required

2.1 Every new recurring residential arrangement begins with a separately priced initial Deep Cleaning. There are no exceptions.

2.2 The initial Deep Cleaning establishes the baseline condition for maintenance. It is performed under the Residential Deep Cleaning Agreement and the Deep Cleaning Scope and Terms.

2.3 Ongoing maintenance begins after the initial Deep Cleaning is completed. The first-visit price and the ongoing per-visit price are separate and are stated in the signed schedule.

2.4 A recurring arrangement is not a discounted continuation of a one-time cleaning. The initial Deep Cleaning price is not reduced because the customer intends to continue service.

## 3. Frequency and Scheduling

3.1 The available maintenance frequencies are weekly, biweekly, and monthly. The signed schedule states the selected frequency, preferred day, arrival window, and first maintenance date.

3.2 Sparkling Standard will use reasonable efforts to keep the same day and arrival window. A holiday, weather event, staffing issue, or property condition may require an adjustment.

3.3 The customer will provide access and a safe working environment for each visit. A missed visit caused by unavailable access may be treated as a failed-access event under Section 19 of the Core Terms.

3.4 If the customer requests a frequency change, Sparkling Standard will confirm the available schedule and any price change in writing before the change takes effect.

## 4. Maintenance Scope

4.1 Ongoing maintenance uses the approved recurring residential checklist: light tidying and organization; dusting high and low; wiping and disinfecting reachable surfaces; baseboard dusting; wall spot-cleaning; blinds, window frames, and sills; vacuuming and mopping floors; replacing trash liners; glass doors and designated focal windows; and kitchen and bathroom maintenance.

4.2 The signed schedule identifies any selected optional work. Work not listed is excluded.

4.3 Maintenance does not include a new Deep Cleaning. If the property condition requires deep work, Sparkling Standard will discuss a separate Deep Cleaning or a revised scope.

## 5. Separate Prices

5.1 The signed schedule states the first-visit Deep Cleaning price and the ongoing per-visit price. The two prices are distinct.

5.2 The ongoing price applies to maintenance visits after the initial Deep Cleaning. It does not change a completed or paid invoice.

5.3 A promotion may apply only to the first visit when its written terms say so. The ongoing price is not discounted by that promotion.

5.4 A price change requires written notice before it takes effect. A price change applies prospectively and does not change a completed or paid visit.

## 6. Condition Changes and Reassessment

6.1 The ongoing price assumes the condition established by the initial Deep Cleaning and disclosed information.

6.2 If the property condition materially changes, including a renovation, new pets, a change in occupancy, excessive buildup, or a condition not previously disclosed, Sparkling Standard may reassess the scope and price.

6.3 Sparkling Standard will confirm any revised scope and price in writing before the changed work is performed. The customer may decline the revised scope.

6.4 Sparkling Standard will not use a reassessment to increase the price for work within the disclosed condition and agreed scope.

## 7. Missed Visits

7.1 If the customer cancels or reschedules a maintenance visit, Section 19 of the Core Terms applies.

7.2 If Sparkling Standard cannot perform a scheduled visit, Sparkling Standard will notify the customer and offer a reasonable replacement date. Sparkling Standard will not charge for a visit it did not perform.

7.3 The customer remains responsible for a visit that was performed or for a failed-access event caused by unavailable access.

7.4 Repeated cancellations or access failures may lead Sparkling Standard to decline future recurring service.

## 8. Customer Termination and Company Suspension

8.1 The customer may end the recurring arrangement with reasonable written notice. The customer remains responsible for completed visits and approved charges.

8.2 Sparkling Standard may suspend or terminate recurring service for unsafe conditions, harassment, repeated access failures, nonpayment of an undisputed invoice, or a request to perform excluded or unlawful work.

8.3 If service is suspended or terminated, Sparkling Standard will confirm the effective date and any outstanding balance in writing.

8.4 There is no hidden minimum term, early-termination penalty, or automatic cancellation fee beyond the confirmed-appointment provisions in Section 19 of the Core Terms.

## 9. Outstanding Balances

9.1 The customer will pay each invoice under Section 17 of the Core Terms. A recurring arrangement does not create credit beyond the signed payment terms.

9.2 An undisputed past-due balance may be handled under Section 18 of the Core Terms. A genuinely disputed amount does not accrue a fee or interest while it is under review.

9.3 Sparkling Standard may pause future visits while an undisputed past-due balance remains unpaid, after providing written notice.

## 10. Access, Privacy, Insurance, and Electronic Signature

10.1 The access terms in Sections 8 through 10 of the Core Terms apply. Sparkling Standard does not retain residential physical house keys.

10.2 The privacy and photograph terms in Section 15 of the Core Terms apply.

10.3 Sparkling Standard Cleaning Co. maintains commercial general liability insurance with limits of $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 general aggregate. Evidence of coverage is available upon request. Coverage is subject to the terms, conditions, exclusions, and limits of the applicable insurance policy. The existence of insurance does not expand or reduce either party's contractual rights or responsibilities.

10.4 The electronic signature and records terms in Section 24 of the Core Terms apply.

## 11. Execution

11.1 The customer and Sparkling Standard execute this Agreement and the signed recurring schedule. The schedule is incorporated into this Agreement.
