# Commercial Key Custody and Access Authorization

**Document ID:** SS-CUST-COM-KEY-2026-11
**Version:** 2026-11-22.remediation.v1
**Status:** Owner review draft — not effective until approved
**Category:** Customer - Commercial Access

## 1. Parties and Facility

1.1 This Commercial Key Custody and Access Authorization is between Sparkling Standard LLC, doing business as Sparkling Standard Cleaning Co., and {{customer.legal_name}} for the commercial facility at {{property.address}}.

1.2 This authorization is incorporated into the Commercial Cleaning Service Agreement and the Core Cleaning Service Terms.

1.3 This authorization does not apply to a residential property. Sparkling Standard does not retain residential physical house keys.

## 2. Authorized Devices

2.1 The customer may authorize Sparkling Standard to receive and temporarily hold the specific key, fob, badge, access card, or other device listed in the signed schedule.

2.2 The schedule identifies each device by a non-secret label or serial number. Full alarm codes, master codes, and passwords are not recorded in this authorization.

2.3 A temporary code, if used, is issued separately and changed after the service ends or when access is revoked.

2.4 Sparkling Standard will not accept an unidentified device, an unrestricted master credential, or a device the customer is not authorized to provide.

## 3. Authorized Personnel and Use

3.1 Only an employee assigned to the facility may use the device, and only for authorized cleaning work.

3.2 The employee must not duplicate, share, lend, photograph, or remove the device from the authorized access process.

3.3 The customer may identify a restricted area or an area that requires an escort. Sparkling Standard will follow the written instruction.

3.4 The device remains the customer's property.

## 4. Custody Record

4.1 Sparkling Standard records each device issued, the authorized employee, date and time issued, purpose, expected return, and return date and time.

4.2 The custodian keeps the record current and reports a missing device immediately.

4.3 A return is recorded when the device is delivered to the customer or the customer's authorized representative.

## 5. Storage and Return

5.1 When not in use, the device is stored in a locked location controlled by the owner or designated custodian.

5.2 The device is returned when the service ends, when the customer requests return, when the employee's authorization ends, or when the service is suspended.

5.3 Sparkling Standard will not retain the device after the authorized purpose ends without a new written authorization.

## 6. Loss, Theft, or Compromise

6.1 Sparkling Standard will report a lost, stolen, or compromised device to the customer promptly.

6.2 The customer may change a code, rekey a lock, or disable a credential. Sparkling Standard will cooperate with the access-control response.

6.3 Sparkling Standard will document the incident and the return or disablement of any remaining device.

## 7. Revocation

7.1 The customer may revoke this authorization in writing at any time.

7.2 Sparkling Standard will stop using the device after it receives and confirms the revocation and will return or disable the device as directed.

7.3 Revocation does not affect a completed service or an approved charge.

## 8. Responsibility

8.1 The customer is responsible for providing a lawful, working access method and for securing cash, controlled substances, weapons, valuables, records, and confidential information.

8.2 Sparkling Standard is responsible for exercising reasonable care in storing, using, and returning the device.

8.3 Responsibility for loss or misuse is governed by the Commercial Cleaning Service Agreement and applicable law. This authorization does not create a broader bailment, an unlimited liability, or an automatic charge.

## 9. Execution

9.1 The customer and Sparkling Standard execute this authorization. The signed schedule identifies each device and the authorized personnel.
