# Church and Facility Cleaning Agreement

**Document ID:** LEG-9F30E030
**Version:** 2026-11-22.remediation.v1
**Status:** Owner review draft — not effective until approved
**Category:** Customer - Church / Facility

## 1. Parties and Incorporation

1.1 This Church and Facility Cleaning Agreement is between Sparkling Standard LLC, doing business as Sparkling Standard Cleaning Co., and {{customer.legal_name}} for the church or facility at {{property.address}}.

1.2 This Agreement incorporates the Core Cleaning Service Terms (the "Core Terms"). The Core Terms control general rights and responsibilities. This service-specific Agreement controls the obligations unique to this service, and the signed service schedule controls the property, scope, date, price, discount, tax, and payment timing. If a provision conflicts, the order of precedence in Section 2 of the Core Terms applies.

1.3 The customer confirms authority to authorize access and approve the scope and charges for the facility.

## 2. Facility Scope

2.1 The signed schedule controls the facility areas, tasks, frequency, access window, supplies, equipment, price, and term.

2.2 The approved scope may include sanctuary seating, floors, rails, high-touch surfaces, aisles, fellowship hall and kitchen-adjacent areas, restrooms, nursery and children's areas, classrooms, offices, corridors, entryways, glass doors, floor care by surface, and seasonal detail work.

2.3 Work not listed in the signed schedule is excluded. A change to the facility, occupancy, or event schedule may require a written scope review.

## 3. Sacramental Items and Candles

3.1 Sparkling Standard may clean sacramental items and carefully move candles when the church authorizes that handling within the agreed scope and insurance.

3.2 The church will identify any item that must not be moved, touched, polished, or cleaned with a particular product.

3.3 Sparkling Standard will use reasonable care and follow the church's written handling instructions. Sparkling Standard will not perform a religious ritual or handle a consecrated item in a manner the church has not authorized.

3.4 If an item requires specialist conservation, restoration, or a hazardous-material procedure, Sparkling Standard will not handle it.

## 4. Excluded Work

4.1 Sparkling Standard does not perform biohazard cleanup, mold remediation, pest control, fire restoration, construction cleanup, hazardous chemical remediation, unsafe height work, or repair.

4.2 The church remains responsible for securing cash, offering records, confidential member information, and any item it does not authorize Sparkling Standard to handle.

4.3 The church will tell Sparkling Standard about a hazardous condition, infestation, or unsafe area before the visit.

## 5. Church Discount

5.1 Eligible church cleaning receives a 10% discount on the cleaning service, applied after the minimum-job rule and under the discount configuration confirmed by Sparkling Standard.

5.2 The discount does not apply to travel, third-party costs, pass-through charges, or a service the signed schedule does not identify as eligible.

5.3 The discount does not change a historical paid invoice and is not a donation or tithe.

5.4 The discount does not stack with another cleaning discount unless the signed schedule or the written promotion terms expressly say it may stack.

## 6. Special Events

6.1 Special-event cleaning requires a separate supplemental scope and price. It is not included by implication.

6.2 The church will provide the event date, areas, expected condition, access window, and any restricted area before the event.

6.3 A same-day or after-hours event request is subject to availability and a written supplemental authorization.

## 7. Privacy and Member Information

7.1 Sparkling Standard will limit access to private offices, member records, and children's areas to the work authorized in the signed schedule.

7.2 The team will not read, copy, photograph, or disclose member records, offering records, or confidential church information.

7.3 Photographs for quality or dispute purposes are handled under Section 15 of the Core Terms and are not published without separate written consent.

7.4 The church will secure confidential records and cash before the visit.

## 8. Scheduling, Access, and Security

8.1 The signed schedule states the service days, hours, access window, and any event blackout.

8.2 The church will provide working access, alarm instructions, and any building requirement.

8.3 Commercial keys, fobs, or access cards are governed by the separate Commercial Key Custody and Access Authorization.

## 9. Price, Payment, and Insurance

9.1 The signed schedule states the price, eligible discount, tax, and total. Church invoices are due Net 15 calendar days from the invoice date unless the signed schedule states another due date.

9.2 Sparkling Standard Cleaning Co. maintains commercial general liability insurance with limits of $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 general aggregate. Evidence of coverage is available upon request. Coverage is subject to the terms, conditions, exclusions, and limits of the applicable insurance policy. The existence of insurance does not expand or reduce either party's contractual rights or responsibilities.

9.3 The electronic signature and records terms in Section 24 of the Core Terms apply.

## 10. Execution

10.1 The church and Sparkling Standard execute this Agreement and the signed facility schedule.
