## 1. Parties and Authority

1.1 Sparkling Standard LLC is a Florida limited liability company doing business as Sparkling Standard Cleaning Co. Its principal contact for this Agreement is admin@sparkling-standard.com or (850) 426-8479.

1.2 The customer is the person or entity identified in the signed service schedule. The customer confirms that it is the property owner, an owner-authorized agent, a tenant authorized to arrange cleaning, or a property manager authorized to bind the owner.

1.3 If the customer arranges service for a property it does not own, the customer confirms authority to grant access, approve the scope, and approve charges for that property. The customer remains responsible for the obligations it accepts in the signed documents.

1.4 Sparkling Standard may rely on the contact information and authority represented in the signed schedule. A person who signs for an entity confirms that the entity has authorized that signature.

1.5 No employee, contractor, or representative may orally waive a term, accept a change to the scope, or bind Sparkling Standard to an additional obligation unless the change is confirmed in writing by an authorized owner.

## 2. Agreement Structure and Precedence

2.1 The controlling documents are: (a) these Core Terms; (b) the applicable service-specific agreement; (c) the customer-specific signed service schedule; and (d) a signed additional-service or payment authorization.

2.2 The documents are read together. A signed service schedule controls the commercial details for that service, including the property, scope, date, arrival window, price, discounts, tax, and payment timing. The service-specific agreement controls obligations unique to that service. The Core Terms control general rights and responsibilities. An additional authorization controls only the additional work or payment it expressly covers.

2.3 If a provision in a later signed document conflicts with an earlier document, the later provision controls only to the extent of the conflict and only if the later document expressly identifies the provision it is changing. A non-waivable legal right controls over any conflicting term.

2.4 A published website page, estimate, proposal, email, text message, or oral statement does not amend the signed documents unless it is incorporated by a signed writing. Marketing descriptions and examples are illustrative and do not expand the signed scope.

2.5 A draft, reference-only, historical, or superseded document is not part of this Agreement and creates no obligation. Only a document incorporated by a signed writing is part of this Agreement.

## 3. Scope of Work

3.1 Sparkling Standard performs only the cleaning and related services described in the signed service schedule and the applicable service-specific agreement. Anything not listed is excluded.

3.2 The signed schedule identifies the service type, property, areas, tasks, frequency, date or period, arrival window, price, selected additions, and any known limitation. The service-specific agreement describes the standard scope for that service.

3.3 Optional work is performed only when selected in writing and priced. Custom work requires a separate written quote or change authorization.

3.4 Sparkling Standard does not promise removal of every stain, mark, odor, mineral deposit, or pre-existing condition. Sparkling Standard protects finishes and follows manufacturer instructions. A surface that is damaged, etched, delaminated, or improperly sealed may not respond to cleaning.

3.5 The customer understands that cleaning is a visual, surface-level service. Sparkling Standard does not perform restoration, repair, construction, mold remediation, pest control, biohazard remediation, or specialist work unless a separate written scope and any required qualification are in place.

## 4. Service-Specific Inclusions and Exclusions

4.1 The applicable service-specific agreement and signed schedule control the inclusions for Deep Cleaning, recurring residential cleaning, move-in/move-out cleaning, short-term rental turnover, commercial cleaning, church or facility cleaning, and any approved specialty service.

4.2 Standard residential cleaning generally includes reachable counters and sinks, appliance exteriors, bathrooms, dusting of reachable surfaces, vacuuming and mopping of floors including edges, bed making when requested, light tidying, switches and door handles, and trash removal with new liners.

4.3 Optional residential work may include inside cabinets, drawers, or closets; inside a refrigerator or oven; interior windows; ground-reachable exterior windows; one load of laundry; bed linen change; dishes; general organization; and pet-hair intensive work, only when selected and priced.

4.4 Carpet extraction, upholstery extraction, pressure washing, garage work, patio work, post-construction cleaning, and restoration work are custom-quote services. They are not included unless a separate written quote identifies the work and price.

4.5 Excluded or hazardous work is described in Section 13. A service-specific agreement may add exclusions that apply to that service.

## 5. Additional Services

5.1 Additional work is performed only after the customer approves the added scope and price in writing. An Additional Service and Add-On Authorization, a signed change order, or a written confirmation from an authorized owner may record the approval.

5.2 If the customer authorizes additional work orally during a visit, Sparkling Standard will confirm the added scope and price in writing as soon as practicable. The customer may decline the additional work before it begins; Sparkling Standard will not perform it without approval.

5.3 An add-on does not change the base service into a different service, extend a fixed appointment beyond the agreed window, or include work outside the stated add-on description.

5.4 Specialty work, custom work, and work requiring equipment or qualification Sparkling Standard does not hold may be declined even if requested.

## 6. Customer Information and Condition Assessment

6.1 The customer will provide accurate information about the property, including square footage, room counts, bathrooms, condition, buildup, pets, access, parking, existing damage, fragile items, and any specialty condition.

6.2 The customer will tell Sparkling Standard before work begins about any condition that may affect safety, access, the scope, the time required, or the price.

6.3 The estimate and schedule depend on the accuracy of the information provided. If the actual condition differs materially, Section 14 applies.

6.4 The customer will not ask Sparkling Standard to rely on a concealed or misleading condition, and will correct information that the customer knows is inaccurate before the appointment.

6.5 Sparkling Standard may use photographs, measurements, and a written condition record to assess the property and document the completed work. Photographs are handled under Section 15.

## 7. Scheduling and Arrival Windows

7.1 A submitted request, estimate, or temporary hold is not a confirmed appointment. An appointment is confirmed only when Sparkling Standard confirms the scope, date, and price, or when a complete paid booking passes every required server verification.

7.2 The first appointment of the day may be scheduled for an exact arrival time. Later appointments use an arrival window to account for travel and the duration of earlier work.

7.3 Published service hours are 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m., seven days per week, Central Time, unless a signed schedule states otherwise.

7.4 A property takes as long as it honestly takes. Sparkling Standard will not rush work to protect a clock. If the agreed scope cannot be completed in the scheduled window, Sparkling Standard will tell the customer and agree on the next step before leaving.

7.5 The customer will provide accurate day-of-service contact information and respond to access questions within a reasonable time. Repeated unresponsiveness may require rescheduling.

7.6 Sparkling Standard may adjust an arrival window for traffic, weather, safety, or an earlier job that requires additional time. Sparkling Standard will communicate a material delay when it is reasonably able to do so.

## 8. Property Access and Security

8.1 The customer will provide working entry instructions, gate or building codes, parking information, alarm instructions, and any building requirements before the appointment, and will keep them current.

8.2 The customer will tell Sparkling Standard about any camera, alarm, locked area, restricted room, or condition that affects access or privacy.

8.3 If an alarm is armed, the customer will disarm it, provide a temporary code, or tell Sparkling Standard not to enter. Sparkling Standard will not bypass a security system, force a door, or remain in a property where it is not authorized to be.

8.4 If access is unavailable, if an authorized contact cannot be reached when presence is required, or if access is withdrawn after arrival, Sparkling Standard will attempt to contact the customer, document the visit, and may apply the failed-access provision in Section 19.

8.5 The customer is responsible for providing lawful access. Sparkling Standard is not responsible for a delay or missed service caused by an access instruction that does not work.

## 9. Residential No-Key Policy

9.1 Sparkling Standard does not retain residential customers' physical house keys.

9.2 Residential access must use a customer-controlled lockbox, a temporary code, customer-present access, an authorized person, or another method documented in the Residential Access Authorization. The customer controls the method and may change it.

9.3 The customer will not leave a key concealed on the property for Sparkling Standard to find. Sparkling Standard will not accept a key left in an unsecured location.

9.4 A temporary code or lockbox code should be changed after the service or when access ends. The customer is responsible for maintaining the device and providing a working method.

9.5 Commercial keys, fobs, badges, and access cards are governed by Section 10 and the separate Commercial Key Custody and Access Authorization. Nothing in this Section authorizes residential key custody.

## 10. Commercial Access Authorization

10.1 A commercial customer may authorize Sparkling Standard to receive and temporarily hold a specific key, fob, badge, access card, or other access device only through a separate signed Commercial Key Custody and Access Authorization.

10.2 The authorization identifies the facility, device, authorized personnel, custody record, storage method, return procedure, and revocation process. Sparkling Standard will not accept an unidentified device or an unrestricted master credential.

10.3 The customer will tell Sparkling Standard promptly about a changed lock, code, alarm, or access rule. The customer remains responsible for securing cash, controlled substances, weapons, valuables, records, and confidential information.

10.4 Sparkling Standard will report a lost, stolen, or compromised device promptly and will cooperate with the customer's access-control response. The customer may revoke access in writing at any time.

10.5 This Section does not create a bailment broader than the custody described in the signed authorization and does not authorize an automatic charge or unlimited liability.

## 11. Customer Responsibilities and Preparation

11.1 The customer will provide a safe working environment, working utilities, and reasonable access to the areas being cleaned.

11.2 The customer will remove or secure cash, jewelry, firearms, medications, financial documents, heirlooms, and other valuables before service.

11.3 The customer will tell Sparkling Standard about fragile, antique, improperly secured, damaged, or unusually delicate items and surfaces before work begins.

11.4 The customer will secure aggressive or uncontrolled animals before the visit. Pets are welcome when they are safe and do not prevent the work from being done properly.

11.5 The customer will not ask the team to perform work that is unsafe, unlawful, outside the agreed scope, or outside Sparkling Standard's insurance or competence.

11.6 Harassment, threats, discrimination, violence, or unsafe conduct toward the team ends the appointment. Sparkling Standard may leave the property and document the reason.

## 12. Pets, Valuables, Fragile Items, and Existing Damage

12.1 The customer is responsible for securing valuables and for identifying fragile or delicate items before cleaning. Sparkling Standard will use reasonable care but cannot guarantee a pre-existing crack, loose fitting, unstable shelf, or improperly installed fixture will not fail during normal cleaning.

12.2 Sparkling Standard will not move a heavy, unstable, or unusually valuable item unless the signed schedule authorizes the move and the item can be moved safely.

12.3 The customer will disclose existing damage, stains, scratches, chips, loose tiles, peeling finishes, or prior repairs that may be affected by cleaning.

12.4 Photographs or a written condition record may be used to distinguish pre-existing damage from damage reported after service.

12.5 Sparkling Standard is not responsible for damage caused by a defective product the customer supplied, an improperly installed fixture, a hidden condition, or an item that the customer did not identify as fragile.

## 13. Unsafe, Hazardous, and Excluded Work

13.1 Sparkling Standard does not perform post-construction cleaning; biohazard or human waste cleanup; severe animal waste; needle or sharps cleanup; crime-scene cleanup; severe hoarding; mold remediation; serious infestations including bedbugs; hazardous chemical remediation; fire restoration; unsafe height or ladder work; or any work requiring specialist certification, equipment, or insurance Sparkling Standard does not hold.

13.2 If a condition requiring specialist work is discovered, Sparkling Standard will stop and tell the customer. Sparkling Standard will not attempt work outside its competence or insurance.

13.3 If conditions are unsafe, threatening, infested, structurally unsound, or outside the agreed scope, Sparkling Standard may stop work immediately and document why.

13.4 The customer will not conceal a hazardous condition or ask the team to handle a substance or situation that requires a specialist.

13.5 Sparkling Standard may decline work that it reasonably believes is unsafe or unlawful, even if the customer offers to pay for it.

## 14. On-Site Reassessment and Written Change Authorization

14.1 If actual conditions differ materially from the information provided, Sparkling Standard may need to reassess before work begins.

14.2 An on-site reassessment is a documented review. It is not a punishment and is not automatically a fee.

14.3 After reassessment, Sparkling Standard may confirm the original scope and price; offer a revised written quote; adjust the scope to fit the agreed amount; reschedule; or decline unsafe, hazardous, or specialist work.

14.4 Sparkling Standard will not charge more than the amount the customer approved. If the customer declines a necessary revised scope before work begins, either party may decline to proceed.

14.5 A change order or Additional Service and Add-On Authorization may be recorded in writing, including email or text, before additional work is performed. If the customer authorizes additional work orally during a visit, Sparkling Standard will confirm the added scope and price in writing as soon as practicable.

14.6 Sparkling Standard will not use a reassessment as a pretext to increase a fixed price for work that is within the disclosed scope and condition.

## 15. Photographs and Privacy

15.1 Photographs uploaded by the customer are private and are used only to prepare and confirm the estimate and cleaning. They are not posted publicly.

15.2 The customer should upload only the areas being cleaned and should not upload identification documents, screens, mail, prescriptions, family photographs, or personal paperwork.

15.3 Sparkling Standard may take before-and-after or condition photographs for quality control, dispute resolution, and internal records. Sparkling Standard will not publish identifiable photographs of the customer's home or belongings for marketing without the customer's separate written consent in the Customer Photo and Marketing Consent.

15.4 Photographs are stored in private storage with access limited to authorized personnel and are retained only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. The customer may ask Sparkling Standard to delete non-essential photographs, subject to records that must be kept for tax, insurance, or dispute purposes.

15.5 Sparkling Standard's handling of personal information is described in the published Privacy Policy. If this Agreement and the Privacy Policy conflict about privacy, the Privacy Policy controls for privacy matters.

15.6 The customer will tell Sparkling Standard before the visit about any area that may not be photographed.

## 16. Pricing, Estimates, and Taxes

16.1 The online estimate is a proposed estimate, not a guaranteed final price. Final scope, condition, access, and unique circumstances can affect the confirmed price.

16.2 The signed service schedule states the confirmed price, selected additions, discount, tax treatment, and payment timing. If a price is marked preliminary or subject to confirmation, it is not a final price until confirmed in writing.

16.3 Sparkling Standard will not charge more than the amount the customer approved unless the customer authorizes a written change.

16.4 Taxes are applied according to the law and the confirmed service location. The invoice identifies the taxable amount, rate, and total when tax applies. A service that is not taxable will not be described as tax-free unless the applicable rule supports that treatment.

16.5 A promotion, account benefit, or gift certificate is applied only under its written terms. A promotion never changes a historical paid invoice, and a gift certificate is payment tender rather than a discount.

## 17. Payments, Invoices, and Receipts

17.1 Payment may be made through the methods Sparkling Standard publishes or agrees in writing. Sparkling Standard does not store full card numbers or security codes in the application.

17.2 Payment processing is performed by Stripe or another disclosed processor. The customer's use of a payment method is also subject to the processor's terms.

17.3 Residential cleaning is due upon completion unless prepaid. Commercial, church, and facility invoices are due Net 15 calendar days from the invoice date unless a signed schedule states another due date.

17.4 A receipt or invoice identifies the service, amount paid or due, tax, discount, and balance. The customer should review it promptly and contact Sparkling Standard about a discrepancy.

17.5 If the customer believes a charge is wrong, the customer should contact Sparkling Standard first so the charge can be investigated. Sparkling Standard will not retaliate against a customer for exercising a legitimate cardholder dispute right.

17.6 Overpayments and duplicate payments are refunded or credited after verification. The customer should not send payment to an account or link that did not come from an authorized Sparkling Standard communication.

17.7 A payment authorization is separate from the service agreement. A saved payment method alone does not authorize an unlimited or recurring charge.

## 18. Late Payment, Interest, and Disputed Balances

18.1 Due dates. Residential cleaning is due upon completion unless prepaid. Commercial, church, and facility invoices are due Net 15 calendar days from the invoice date unless a signed customer-specific schedule states another due date.

18.2 Grace period. A payment is not late until 30 calendar days after its contractual due date.

18.3 Late fee. After the grace period, Sparkling Standard may assess one $20 late fee on an undisputed past-due balance where lawful and disclosed. The fee is not assessed more than once on the same balance.

18.4 Interest. After the grace period, unpaid principal may accrue simple interest at 15% per year. Interest is calculated using actual days elapsed and a 365-day denominator. There is no compounding, and no interest is charged on late fees or on accrued interest.

18.5 Disputed balances. A genuinely disputed amount does not accrue a late fee or interest while Sparkling Standard is reviewing a documented billing correction. The undisputed portion remains payable.

18.6 Partial payments. Partial payments and approved credits reduce the principal balance on which interest may be calculated. Payments are applied in the order Sparkling Standard discloses on the invoice or, if not disclosed, to the oldest undisputed balance.

18.7 Legal limits. If applicable law sets a lower maximum fee, interest rate, or charge, the lower limit controls. The late fee and interest are not intended as a penalty.

18.8 No unauthorized charge. A stored payment method is not charged merely because an invoice is overdue. A separate signed payment authorization is required.

## 19. Cancellation, Rescheduling, and Failed Access

19.1 Cancellation by the customer. The customer may cancel or reschedule by contacting Sparkling Standard using the phone, email, website contact form, or another method Sparkling Standard has published. The customer should keep the confirmation Sparkling Standard sends.

19.2 Notice periods and fees. The following maximum amounts apply to a customer-requested cancellation or rescheduling of a confirmed appointment:

- 48 hours or more before the appointment: no fee.
- 24 hours but less than 48 hours before the appointment: up to $20.
- Less than 24 hours before the appointment: up to $40.
- Failed access after arrival: up to $40.
19.3 Cost limitation. Any amount charged under Section 19.2 is limited to the lesser of the stated maximum and Sparkling Standard's documented reasonable unrecoverable costs, which may include allocated labor, travel, a lost slot, and preparation time. The amount is a reasonable estimate of loss, not a penalty.

19.4 No stacking. Sparkling Standard will not charge both a cancellation fee and a failed-access fee for the same event.

19.5 Courtesy waiver. Sparkling Standard may waive one late-cancellation fee per customer in any rolling twelve-month period. A waiver is discretionary and does not create a continuing right.

19.6 Exceptions. Sparkling Standard will not charge a cancellation fee when Sparkling Standard cancels, when a documented emergency, serious illness, severe weather, or genuine hardship prevents performance, or when a fee would violate applicable law. The customer may be asked for a brief explanation; no sensitive medical records are required.

19.7 Statutory rights. Nothing in this Agreement limits a cancellation, rescission, refund, or notice right that applicable law does not allow the customer to waive. A fee will not be charged during a statutory cancellation period.

19.8 No automatic charge. A cancellation or failed-access fee is not charged automatically from a stored payment method. It is invoiced or separately authorized in writing.

19.9 Repeated events. Repeated cancellations, repeated access failures, or abusive scheduling conduct may lead Sparkling Standard to decline future bookings.

## 20. Satisfaction and Correction

20.1 If something within the agreed cleaning scope was missed, the customer should report it promptly and identify the area and task. Sparkling Standard will review the report and provide reasonable corrective cleaning at no additional charge for verified missed work.

20.2 This correction procedure is not an unconditional refund promise and does not extend to unrelated work, restoration, repair, or conditions outside the scope.

20.3 The customer should allow Sparkling Standard a reasonable opportunity to inspect and correct the work before arranging a third-party remedy. Sparkling Standard will respond in writing where appropriate.

20.4 A complaint about damage is handled under Section 21. A complaint about cleaning quality is handled under this Section.

20.5 Sparkling Standard may document the correction with photographs and a completion note.

## 21. Damage Reporting, Investigation, and Responsibility

21.1 Sparkling Standard works carefully and treats every property with respect. The customer is responsible for identifying fragile, antique, improperly secured, damaged, or unusually delicate items and surfaces before work begins, and for securing valuables.

21.2 Sparkling Standard is responsible for damage caused by its own negligent work within the agreed scope. Sparkling Standard is not responsible for pre-existing damage, ordinary wear, improperly installed fixtures, damage caused by a product the customer supplied, or a condition Sparkling Standard could not reasonably see or access. This Section does not waive liability for Sparkling Standard's own negligence.

21.3 If the customer believes Sparkling Standard damaged an item, the customer should tell Sparkling Standard promptly, preserve the item and any photographs, and avoid repair or disposal before Sparkling Standard can inspect it when inspection is reasonably possible.

21.4 Sparkling Standard will investigate, document the claim, and work toward a fair resolution. The customer and Sparkling Standard will cooperate in good faith with an insurance or damage investigation, including photographs, receipts where available, and a written description of the loss.

21.5 Nothing in this Agreement limits a right that cannot legally be limited.

## 22. Insurance

Sparkling Standard Cleaning Co. maintains commercial general liability insurance with limits of $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 general aggregate. Evidence of coverage is available upon request. Coverage is subject to the terms, conditions, exclusions, and limits of the applicable insurance policy. The existence of insurance does not expand or reduce either party's contractual rights or responsibilities.

22.1 The insurance described in this Section is commercial general liability insurance maintained by Sparkling Standard. It does not create coverage for a customer's separate property, business interruption, or personal injury claim beyond the applicable policy.

22.2 A certificate or evidence of coverage may be requested from Sparkling Standard. The certificate is evidence of insurance, not a change to the policy.

## 23. Suspension and Termination

23.1 Either party may terminate a recurring arrangement with reasonable written notice. The customer remains responsible for services already performed and approved charges.

23.2 Sparkling Standard may suspend or terminate service immediately for unsafe conditions, harassment, threats, repeated access failures, nonpayment of an undisputed invoice, or a request to perform excluded or unlawful work.

23.3 The customer may suspend or terminate future service at any time, subject to the cancellation provisions for a confirmed appointment and payment for completed work.

23.4 Termination does not erase record-retention obligations, payment obligations for completed work, or rights that survive by law.

23.5 If service is terminated, Sparkling Standard will return or disable any commercial access device under the signed custody authorization.
