# Customer Photo and Marketing Consent

**Document ID:** LEG-6467D94C
**Version:** 2026-11-22.remediation.v1
**Status:** Owner review draft — not effective until approved
**Category:** Customer - Optional Consent

## 1. Voluntary Consent

1.1 The customer may separately consent to Sparkling Standard using selected photographs of the cleaned areas for marketing. Consent is voluntary and is not required to receive cleaning service.

1.2 The customer may decline this consent without affecting the service, price, or scheduling.

## 2. Scope of Consent

2.1 This consent covers only the specific photographs and uses identified in the signed consent. It does not cover a different property, a different use, or a photograph the customer has not approved.

2.2 Approved uses may include the Sparkling Standard website, social media, printed marketing, and a review or testimonial page.

2.3 Sparkling Standard will not sell the photographs or license them to an unrelated third party.

## 3. Exclusions

3.1 This consent does not cover identification documents, mail, screens, prescriptions, family photographs, street numbers, alarms, license plates, private areas, or any image the customer has not approved.

3.2 The customer will identify any area that may not be photographed.

3.3 Sparkling Standard will not publish a photograph that reveals private information or a person who has not consented.

## 4. Withdrawal

4.1 The customer may withdraw marketing consent in writing for future uses.

4.2 Sparkling Standard will stop using the photograph for a new publication after it receives and confirms the withdrawal. Withdrawal does not require the removal of a printed item already distributed or a record retained for legal purposes.

4.3 The customer may ask Sparkling Standard to remove a published photograph from a platform Sparkling Standard controls, subject to reasonable technical limitations.

## 5. Storage and Retention

5.1 Photographs are stored in private storage with access limited to authorized personnel.

5.2 Sparkling Standard retains the consent record and a copy of the approved photograph for as long as needed to document the consent or as required by law.

## 6. No Payment or Endorsement

6.1 Sparkling Standard does not promise payment, a discount, or a benefit for this consent unless a separate written agreement says so.

6.2 The customer is not required to provide a testimonial or endorsement.

## 7. Execution

7.1 The customer and Sparkling Standard execute this consent. The signed consent identifies the approved photographs and uses.
