# Recurring Payment Authorization

**Document ID:** LEG-F99EEE42
**Version:** 2026-11-22.remediation.v1
**Status:** Owner review draft — not effective until approved
**Category:** Customer - Payment Authorization

## 1. Status and Scope

1.1 This Recurring Payment Authorization is optional. It applies only when Sparkling Standard has enabled recurring saved-method charging and the customer has signed this authorization for a specific recurring arrangement.

1.2 This authorization does not apply to a one-time after-service charge. A separate Saved Payment Method Authorization is required for that purpose.

1.3 A saved payment method alone does not create unlimited authorization.

## 2. Authorization Elements

2.1 The signed authorization states the customer, property, covered recurring service, frequency, per-charge maximum, total authorization period or expiration, and the first charge date.

2.2 Sparkling Standard will not charge more than the authorized per-charge maximum or for a service the customer did not authorize.

2.3 A price change requires a new written authorization before the changed amount is charged.

2.4 The customer will receive a receipt or invoice for each charge.

## 3. Payment Timing and Notification

3.1 The signed authorization states whether a charge occurs before the visit, at completion, or on a stated billing date.

3.2 Sparkling Standard will provide a billing notice before the first charge and after a change to the authorized amount or timing.

3.3 A failed payment may be retried only under the processor's rules and applicable law. Sparkling Standard will not repeatedly retry a failed charge in a way that creates duplicate or unauthorized charges.

## 4. Revocation

4.1 The customer may revoke this authorization in writing at any time.

4.2 Revocation applies to future charges. It does not affect a charge already earned for completed work or a charge the customer separately approved.

4.3 Sparkling Standard will confirm the revocation and update the saved-method record promptly.

## 5. Disputes

5.1 The customer should contact Sparkling Standard promptly about a charge the customer believes is wrong.

5.2 Sparkling Standard will investigate and correct a verified error. A genuinely disputed amount will not be charged again while it is under review.

5.3 Nothing in this authorization limits a cardholder dispute right.

## 6. No Unauthorized Charge

6.1 Sparkling Standard will not charge a stored method merely because an invoice is overdue.

6.2 Sparkling Standard will not use a saved method for a different customer, property, or service.

6.3 A charge above the authorized maximum requires a new signed authorization.

## 7. Records

7.1 Sparkling Standard retains the signed authorization, version, timestamp, related invoice, and receipt. Full card numbers and security codes are not stored.

## 8. Execution

8.1 The customer and Sparkling Standard execute this authorization. The customer confirms that the maximum, service, and timing are accurately stated.
