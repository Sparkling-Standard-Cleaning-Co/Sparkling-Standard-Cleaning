# Deep Cleaning Scope and Terms

**Document ID:** LEG-633AE35B
**Version:** 2026-11-22.remediation.v1
**Status:** Owner review draft — not effective until approved
**Category:** Customer - Service Schedule

## 1. Purpose and Controlling Documents

1.1 This Deep Cleaning Scope and Terms is a service schedule under the Core Cleaning Service Terms and the Residential Deep Cleaning Agreement. It is incorporated into those documents.

1.2 If this schedule conflicts with the Core Terms or the Residential Deep Cleaning Agreement, the order of precedence in Section 2 of the Core Terms applies. The signed schedule controls the property, selected work, date, and price.

## 2. Approved Deep Cleaning Checklist

2.1 The following checklist is the approved Deep Cleaning scope, subject to safe access and the condition of the property:

- Full standard cleaning scope, including reachable counters and sinks, appliance exteriors, bathrooms, dusting of reachable surfaces, vacuuming and mopping floors including edges, switches, door handles, and trash removal with new liners.
- Baseboards hand-wiped rather than only dry-dusted.
- Doors, door frames, and jambs detailed.
- Switch plates, outlet covers, and high-touch points wiped.
- Wall spot-cleaning where the finish allows.
- Damp dusting where the surface calls for it.
- Blinds and window sills detailed.
- Cabinet fronts cleaned with attention to edges and handles.
- Kitchen and bathroom deep detail, including corners, sound grout lines, fixtures, and reachable buildup.
- Interior glass and reachable window tracks when selected.
- Final detailed walkthrough against this checklist.
## 3. Selected Optional Work

3.1 Optional work is included only when the signed schedule selects it and states the price. Optional work may include inside cabinets, drawers, or closets; inside a refrigerator or oven; interior windows; ground-reachable exterior windows; laundry; bed linen change; dishes; organization; and pet-hair intensive work.

3.2 An optional item does not include repair, restoration, or work outside its description.

## 4. Condition and Time

4.1 The scheduled duration is an estimate based on the information provided. A property with heavy buildup, excessive clutter, or a condition different from the information provided may require a documented reassessment.

4.2 Sparkling Standard may confirm the original scope and price, provide a revised written quote, adjust the scope, reschedule, or decline unsafe or specialist work. Sparkling Standard will not charge more than the approved amount.

## 5. Limitations

5.1 Sparkling Standard does not promise removal of every stain, discoloration, odor, mineral deposit, or pre-existing condition.

5.2 Sparkling Standard does not perform restoration, repair, caulking, grout replacement, paint touch-up, refinishing, biohazard cleanup, mold remediation, pest control, or unsafe height work.

5.3 The customer will identify fragile, antique, damaged, or unusually delicate items and surfaces before work begins.

## 6. Completion and Correction

6.1 Sparkling Standard performs a final walkthrough against this checklist and documents completion.

6.2 A verified missed item within the agreed scope will be corrected at no additional charge under Section 20 of the Core Terms.

6.3 A damage claim is handled under Section 21 of the Core Terms.

## 7. Price and Payment

7.1 The signed schedule states the price, selected optional work, discount, tax, and total. Residential payment is due upon completion unless prepaid.

7.2 A promotion or gift certificate applies only under its written terms.

## 8. Execution

8.1 This schedule is incorporated into the Residential Deep Cleaning Agreement when signed by the customer and Sparkling Standard.
