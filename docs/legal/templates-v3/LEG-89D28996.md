# Cancellation and Rescheduling Terms

**Document ID:** LEG-89D28996
**Version:** 2026-11-22.remediation.v1
**Status:** Owner review draft — not effective until approved
**Category:** Customer - Policy

## 1. Scope

1.1 These Cancellation and Rescheduling Terms apply to a confirmed cleaning appointment unless a signed service schedule states a different term that is permitted by law.

1.2 These terms are incorporated into the Core Cleaning Service Terms.

## 2. Customer Cancellation and Rescheduling

19.2 Notice periods and fees. The following maximum amounts apply to a customer-requested cancellation or rescheduling of a confirmed appointment:

- 48 hours or more before the appointment: no fee.
- 24 hours but less than 48 hours before the appointment: up to $20.
- Less than 24 hours before the appointment: up to $40.
- Failed access after arrival: up to $40.
19.3 Cost limitation. Any amount charged under Section 19.2 is limited to the lesser of the stated maximum and Sparkling Standard's documented reasonable unrecoverable costs, which may include allocated labor, travel, a lost slot, and preparation time. The amount is a reasonable estimate of loss, not a penalty.

## 3. Company Cancellation

3.1 Sparkling Standard will not charge a cancellation fee when Sparkling Standard cancels.

3.2 If Sparkling Standard cannot perform a confirmed appointment, Sparkling Standard will notify the customer and offer a reasonable replacement date.

3.3 A refund or credit for a prepaid service that Sparkling Standard cannot perform will be handled under Section 17 of the Core Terms.

## 4. Documentation and Records

4.1 Sparkling Standard will document the appointment time, the notice received, the travel or preparation actually incurred, and the amount assessed.

4.2 The customer may request a written explanation of a cancellation or failed-access amount.

## 5. Statutory Rights

5.1 Nothing in these terms limits a cancellation, rescission, refund, or notice right that applicable law does not allow the customer to waive.

5.2 A fee will not be charged during a statutory cancellation period.

## 6. No Automatic Charge

6.1 A cancellation or failed-access amount is not charged automatically from a stored payment method.

6.2 The amount is invoiced or separately authorized in writing.

## 7. Repeated Events

7.1 Repeated cancellations, repeated access failures, or abusive scheduling conduct may lead Sparkling Standard to decline future bookings.

## 8. Execution

8.1 These terms are incorporated into the Core Terms and the applicable service agreement when signed or accepted in the approved electronic signing session.
