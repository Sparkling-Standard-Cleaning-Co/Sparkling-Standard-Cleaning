# Property Manager / Real Estate Master Service Agreement

**Document ID:** LEG-PM-2026-11
**Version:** 2026-11-22.remediation.v1
**Status:** Owner review draft — not effective until approved
**Category:** Customer - Property Management

## 1. Parties and Roles

1.1 This Property Manager / Real Estate Master Service Agreement is between Sparkling Standard LLC, doing business as Sparkling Standard Cleaning Co., and {{customer.legal_name}} (the "property manager" or "agent").

1.2 This Agreement is a master agreement for cleaning and turnover services at properties identified in one or more signed property schedules.

1.3 Each property schedule identifies the property owner, the authorized agent, the contracting party, the responsible payer, the service scope, the access method, the price, and the billing terms.

1.4 The property manager confirms authority to arrange service and approve charges for each property it adds. If the property manager is not the owner, the property manager confirms that the owner has authorized the arrangement.

## 2. Property Schedules and Authority

2.1 A property is not covered until a signed property schedule identifies it. A verbal request or a listing address alone does not create coverage.

2.2 The property manager will identify the person authorized to approve additional work, access changes, and invoices for each property.

2.3 The property manager will tell Sparkling Standard promptly if ownership, management authority, occupancy, or the responsible payer changes.

2.4 Sparkling Standard may rely on the authority represented in the signed schedule until it receives written notice of a change.

## 3. Scope and Scheduling

3.1 The property schedule states the service type, areas, tasks, frequency, turnover pattern, access window, and price.

3.2 The service-specific agreement for the selected service (residential, move-in/move-out, STR turnover, commercial, or church) applies to the work at that property.

3.3 A turnover or move cleaning requires a workable window and the property condition described in the applicable service-specific agreement.

3.4 If the property condition or access differs materially, Sparkling Standard may reassess and provide a written revised scope and price.

## 4. Access, Turnover, and Security

4.1 The property schedule identifies the access method and the person responsible for providing it. Sparkling Standard does not retain residential physical house keys.

4.2 Commercial keys, fobs, badges, or access cards are governed by the separate Commercial Key Custody and Access Authorization.

4.3 The property manager will provide current tenant, guest, vendor, alarm, parking, and building instructions.

4.4 The property manager will ensure the property is vacant or that remaining belongings are disclosed before a move or turnover cleaning.

4.5 A failed access or an unprepared property may be treated under Section 19 of the Core Terms.

## 5. Scope Changes

5.1 Additional work requires a written change authorization or an updated property schedule that states the scope and price.

5.2 The property manager will identify who may approve a change for each property. Sparkling Standard will not rely on a tenant or guest to approve the owner's charge.

5.3 Sparkling Standard will not perform open-ended additional work without an approved price or rate.

## 6. Invoicing and Payer

6.1 The property schedule identifies the responsible payer and billing contact. The property manager remains responsible for payment it authorized unless the schedule expressly identifies a different payer.

6.2 Invoices are due under the signed schedule or, if none is stated, Net 15 calendar days from the invoice date.

6.3 The invoice identifies the property, service, date, scope, amount, tax, and any approved addition.

6.4 A billing dispute is handled under Section 18 of the Core Terms. The undisputed portion remains payable.

## 7. Completion Records

7.1 Sparkling Standard may provide completion notes, photographs, or an issue report for a property when the signed schedule requires them.

7.2 Photographs are handled under Section 15 of the Core Terms and are not published without separate marketing consent.

7.3 The property manager will tell Sparkling Standard about a private area, tenant belonging, or document that must not be photographed.

## 8. Damage and Incident Notification

8.1 Sparkling Standard will report a material incident or observed property damage promptly to the property manager.

8.2 A damage claim is handled under Section 21 of the Core Terms. The property manager will preserve the item and provide access for inspection when reasonably possible.

8.3 The property manager will report a hazardous condition, biohazard, pest issue, or unsafe area before the visit.

## 9. Termination of Property-Specific Services

9.1 The property manager may terminate service for a property with written notice. The notice should identify the property and the effective date.

9.2 Sparkling Standard may suspend or terminate service for a property for unsafe conditions, repeated access failures, nonpayment of an undisputed invoice, or a request to perform excluded or unlawful work.

9.3 The property manager remains responsible for work performed and approved charges before the effective date.

9.4 On termination, any commercial access device is returned or disabled under the custody authorization.

## 10. Insurance and Electronic Signature

10.1 Sparkling Standard Cleaning Co. maintains commercial general liability insurance with limits of $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 general aggregate. Evidence of coverage is available upon request. Coverage is subject to the terms, conditions, exclusions, and limits of the applicable insurance policy. The existence of insurance does not expand or reduce either party's contractual rights or responsibilities.

10.2 The electronic signature and records terms in Section 24 of the Core Terms apply.

## 11. Execution

11.1 The property manager and Sparkling Standard execute this master Agreement and each property schedule.
