# Residential Access Authorization

**Document ID:** LEG-A636DDB4
**Version:** 2026-11-22.remediation.v1
**Status:** Owner review draft — not effective until approved
**Category:** Customer - Access

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This Residential Access Authorization records the customer-approved access method for the residential property at {{property.address}}.

1.2 This authorization is incorporated into the Core Cleaning Service Terms and the applicable residential service agreement.

1.3 Sparkling Standard does not retain residential customers' physical house keys under any circumstance.

## 2. Approved Access Methods

2.1 The customer will select and describe an approved method:

- Customer-present access.
- A customer-controlled lockbox.
- A temporary door or alarm code that the customer changes after service.
- Access through a building concierge, front desk, or authorized person.
- Another method described in the signed schedule.
2.2 The customer is responsible for providing a working method and for keeping it current.

2.3 Sparkling Standard will not accept a key concealed on the property, a key left with an unauthorized person, or an unidentified device.

## 3. Instructions

3.1 The customer will provide entry instructions, gate or building codes, parking instructions, alarm instructions, and any building requirement before the appointment.

3.2 The customer will identify any locked area, restricted room, camera, or condition that affects access or privacy.

3.3 The customer will provide a day-of-service contact who can respond to an access question.

## 4. Alarm and Security

4.1 If an alarm is armed, the customer will disarm it, provide a temporary code, or tell Sparkling Standard not to enter.

4.2 Sparkling Standard will not bypass a security system, force a door, disable a camera, or remain in a property where it is not authorized to be.

4.3 The customer will tell Sparkling Standard immediately if a code, lock, alarm, or access rule changes.

## 5. Temporary Codes and Devices

5.1 A temporary code should be unique to the service and changed after the service or when access ends.

5.2 The customer should not provide a permanent master code, banking credential, or account password.

5.3 If a temporary device is used, the customer will record its return or disable it after the service.

## 6. Commercial Access

6.1 Commercial keys, fobs, badges, and access cards require the separate Commercial Key Custody and Access Authorization.

6.2 This residential authorization does not authorize commercial key or badge custody.

## 7. Lost or Compromised Access

7.1 The customer will report a lost, stolen, or compromised device or code promptly.

7.2 Sparkling Standard will report a lost or compromised device to the customer promptly and will cooperate with the customer's access-control response.

7.3 The customer may change a code or rekey a lock. Sparkling Standard will not charge for a rekey unless the loss was caused by Sparkling Standard's negligent handling.

## 8. Revocation

8.1 The customer may revoke this authorization in writing at any time.

8.2 Revocation applies to future access and does not affect a completed service or an approved charge.

8.3 Sparkling Standard will stop using the revoked method after it receives and confirms the revocation.

## 9. Responsibility

9.1 The customer is responsible for the security of the property and for providing lawful access.

9.2 Sparkling Standard is responsible for using the authorized method only for the agreed service and for reporting a known compromise.

9.3 This authorization does not create a bailment, an unlimited liability, or an automatic charge.

## 10. Execution

10.1 The customer and Sparkling Standard execute this authorization. The customer confirms that the access method described in the signed schedule is authorized.
