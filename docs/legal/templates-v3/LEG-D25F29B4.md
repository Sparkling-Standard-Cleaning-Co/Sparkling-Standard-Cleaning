# Commercial Cleaning Service Agreement

**Document ID:** LEG-D25F29B4
**Version:** 2026-11-22.remediation.v1
**Status:** Owner review draft — not effective until approved
**Category:** Customer - Commercial

## 1. Parties and Master Agreement

1.1 This Commercial Cleaning Service Agreement is between Sparkling Standard LLC, doing business as Sparkling Standard Cleaning Co., and {{customer.legal_name}} for the facility or facilities identified in one or more signed facility schedules.

1.2 This Agreement incorporates the Core Cleaning Service Terms (the "Core Terms"). The Core Terms control general rights and responsibilities. This service-specific Agreement controls the obligations unique to this service, and the signed service schedule controls the property, scope, date, price, discount, tax, and payment timing. If a provision conflicts, the order of precedence in Section 2 of the Core Terms applies.

1.3 This Agreement is a master agreement. Each facility schedule identifies the facility address, areas, tasks, frequency, access window, supplies, equipment, price, term, and any site-specific requirement.

1.4 A facility schedule becomes part of this Agreement when signed by both parties. A facility not listed in a signed schedule is not covered.

## 2. Scope and Frequency

2.1 Sparkling Standard performs only the work in the signed facility schedule. Anything not listed is excluded.

2.2 The facility schedule states the service frequency, days, hours, and any blackout or restricted period.

2.3 A change to the facility, square footage, restroom count, occupancy, flooring, or use may require a written scope and price review.

2.4 The customer will tell Sparkling Standard about a construction project, renovation, special event, or condition that materially changes the cleaning requirement.

## 3. Supplies and Equipment

3.1 The signed schedule states whether Sparkling Standard supplies standard cleaning chemicals, consumables, and equipment, or whether the customer supplies them.

3.2 Customer-supplied products must be suitable for the surface and safe for the team. Sparkling Standard may decline a product that is unsafe, unlabeled, incompatible, or prohibited.

3.3 Specialty equipment, carpet extraction, floor machines, lifts, and pressure washing are included only when the signed schedule says so.

3.4 Consumable restocking is included only when the signed schedule identifies the items and the responsibility for purchase.

## 4. Access and Security

4.1 The customer will provide a working access method, building requirements, alarm instructions, parking, and any security check-in procedure.

4.2 Commercial keys, fobs, badges, and access cards are governed by the separate Commercial Key Custody and Access Authorization. Sparkling Standard will not accept an unidentified or unrestricted master credential.

4.3 The customer remains responsible for securing cash, controlled substances, weapons, valuables, records, and confidential information.

4.4 Sparkling Standard will not bypass a security system, force a door, or remain in a restricted area without authorization.

4.5 The customer will notify Sparkling Standard promptly of a changed lock, code, alarm, or access rule.

## 5. Authorized Changes

5.1 Additional work requires a written change authorization or a new facility schedule that states the added scope and price.

5.2 A site supervisor may request a change, but the change is not binding unless the customer's authorized representative and Sparkling Standard confirm it in writing.

5.3 Sparkling Standard will not perform open-ended additional work without an approved price or rate.

## 6. Service Quality and Correction

6.1 Sparkling Standard performs the work to the standard in the signed schedule and will address a verified missed item under Section 20 of the Core Terms.

6.2 The customer will report a quality issue promptly and identify the area and task. Sparkling Standard will inspect and correct verified missed work.

6.3 A correction visit does not include restoration, repair, or work outside the agreed scope.

## 7. Invoices and Payment Terms

7.1 Commercial invoices are due Net 15 calendar days from the invoice date unless a signed facility schedule states another due date.

7.2 The invoice identifies the facility, service period, scope, amount, tax, and any approved addition.

7.3 The customer will identify a billing contact and a purchase order or approval requirement before service begins. A purchase order requirement does not delay payment for work the customer authorized.

7.4 Late payment and disputed balances are governed by Section 18 of the Core Terms.

## 8. Damage and Incident Reporting

8.1 Sparkling Standard will report a material incident, injury, security issue, or observed property damage promptly.

8.2 A damage claim is handled under Section 21 of the Core Terms. The customer will preserve the item and provide access for inspection when reasonably possible.

8.3 The customer will report a hazardous condition, biohazard, pest issue, or unsafe area before the visit. Sparkling Standard may stop work and document the condition.

## 9. Suspension and Termination

9.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement or a facility schedule with the notice stated in the schedule or, if none is stated, 30 calendar days' written notice.

9.2 Sparkling Standard may suspend or terminate service immediately for unsafe conditions, harassment, threats, repeated access failures, nonpayment of an undisputed invoice, or a request to perform excluded or unlawful work.

9.3 The customer remains responsible for work performed and approved charges before the effective date.

9.4 On termination, any commercial access device is returned or disabled under the custody authorization.

## 10. Insurance and Electronic Signature

10.1 Sparkling Standard Cleaning Co. maintains commercial general liability insurance with limits of $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 general aggregate. Evidence of coverage is available upon request. Coverage is subject to the terms, conditions, exclusions, and limits of the applicable insurance policy. The existence of insurance does not expand or reduce either party's contractual rights or responsibilities.

10.2 The electronic signature and records terms in Section 24 of the Core Terms apply.

## 11. Execution

11.1 The customer and Sparkling Standard execute this master Agreement and each facility schedule.
