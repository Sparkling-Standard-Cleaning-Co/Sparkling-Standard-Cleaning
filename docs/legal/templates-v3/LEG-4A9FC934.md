## 1. Parties and Incorporation

1.1 This Residential Deep Cleaning Agreement is between Sparkling Standard LLC, doing business as Sparkling Standard Cleaning Co. ("Sparkling Standard"), and {{customer.legal_name}} (the "customer") for the property at {{property.address}}.

1.2 This Agreement incorporates the Core Cleaning Service Terms (the "Core Terms"). The Core Terms control general rights and responsibilities. 1.3 The customer confirms authority to arrange service for the property and to approve the scope and charges in the signed schedule.

## 2. Service Definition

2.1 A Deep Cleaning is a one-time, detailed reset of a residential property. It is the only standalone one-time residential cleaning service Sparkling Standard offers.

2.2 A Deep Cleaning is required before a new recurring residential maintenance arrangement begins. The initial Deep Cleaning is separately priced from the ongoing maintenance visits.

2.3 A Deep Cleaning is not a restoration, construction cleanup, biohazard cleanup, or specialist remediation service.

## 3. Included Scope

3.1 Subject to safe access and the condition of the property, a Deep Cleaning includes the standard residential cleaning tasks in Section 4.2 of the Core Terms and the detailed work below.

3.2 Detailed scope:

- Full standard cleaning scope for reachable surfaces, floors, bathrooms, kitchen exteriors, switches, door handles, and trash removal.
- Baseboards hand-wiped rather than only dry-dusted.
- Doors, door frames, and jambs detailed.
- Switch plates, outlet covers, and high-touch points wiped.
- Wall spot-cleaning where the finish allows and the mark can be removed without damaging the surface.
- Damp dusting where the surface calls for it.
- Blinds and window sills detailed.
- Cabinet fronts cleaned with attention to edges and handles.
- Kitchen and bathroom deep detail, including corners, grout lines that are sound, fixtures, and reachable buildup.
- Interior glass and reachable window tracks when selected in the schedule.
- A final detailed walkthrough against the signed checklist.
3.3 The signed schedule identifies any selected optional work. Work not listed in the schedule is excluded.

## 4. Optional Work

4.1 Optional work may include inside cabinets, drawers, or closets; inside a refrigerator or oven; interior windows; ground-reachable exterior windows; one load of laundry; bed linen change; dishes; general organization; and pet-hair intensive work.

4.2 Optional work is performed only when selected in the signed schedule and priced. An add-on does not expand the Deep Cleaning into a different service or include work outside the add-on description.

4.3 Carpet extraction, upholstery extraction, pressure washing, garage work, patio work, post-construction cleaning, and restoration work require a separate written quote.

## 5. Exclusions and Limitations

5.1 The excluded and hazardous work in Section 13 of the Core Terms applies. Sparkling Standard does not perform biohazard, human or animal waste, needle or sharps cleanup, crime-scene cleanup, mold remediation, severe infestation work, hazardous chemical remediation, fire restoration, or unsafe height or ladder work.

5.2 Sparkling Standard does not promise removal of every stain, discoloration, odor, mineral deposit, or pre-existing condition. Permanent stains, etching, hard-water damage, failed finishes, and damage that existed before service may remain.

5.3 Sparkling Standard will protect finishes and follow manufacturer instructions. A customer-supplied product may be used only when the customer confirms it is suitable for the surface and Sparkling Standard can use it safely.

5.4 Deep Cleaning does not include repair, caulking, grout replacement, paint touch-up, refinishing, restoration, or replacement of a damaged item.

## 6. Condition Assessment and Reassessment

6.1 The customer will provide accurate information about the property's size, rooms, bathrooms, condition, buildup, pets, access, and any specialty condition.

6.2 A Deep Cleaning takes substantially longer than maintenance cleaning. The scheduled duration is an estimate based on the information provided.

6.3 If the actual condition differs materially, Sparkling Standard may perform the documented reassessment in Section 14 of the Core Terms before work begins. Sparkling Standard may confirm the original scope and price, provide a revised written quote, adjust the scope to the agreed amount, reschedule, or decline unsafe or specialist work.

6.4 Sparkling Standard will not charge more than the amount the customer approved. If the customer declines a necessary revised scope before work begins, either party may decline to proceed.

## 7. Delicate Surfaces and Customer Items

7.1 The customer will identify fragile, antique, improperly secured, damaged, or unusually delicate items and surfaces before work begins.

7.2 Sparkling Standard will use reasonable care but cannot guarantee that a pre-existing crack, loose fitting, unstable shelf, or improperly installed fixture will not fail during normal cleaning.

7.3 Sparkling Standard will not move a heavy, unstable, or unusually valuable item unless the signed schedule authorizes the move and the item can be moved safely.

7.4 The customer is responsible for securing cash, jewelry, firearms, medications, financial documents, heirlooms, and other valuables.

## 8. Scheduling and Access

8.1 The signed schedule states the service date and arrival window. A requested date or time is a preference until confirmed.

8.2 The customer will provide the access method in the Residential Access Authorization. Sparkling Standard does not retain residential physical house keys.

8.3 If access is unavailable after arrival, Sparkling Standard will attempt to contact the customer and may apply the failed-access provision in Section 19 of the Core Terms.

8.4 The customer will provide working utilities and a safe working environment. If water, electricity, or safe access is unavailable, the visit may be rescheduled.

## 9. Price, Payment, and Taxes

9.1 The signed schedule states the Deep Cleaning price, selected optional work, discount, tax treatment, and total.

9.2 Residential payment is due upon completion unless prepaid. A gift certificate is payment tender, not a discount.

9.3 Taxes are applied according to the law and the confirmed service location. The invoice identifies any taxable amount, rate, and total.

9.4 A promotion or account benefit applies only under its written terms and never changes a historical paid invoice.

## 10. Quality Review and Correction

10.1 Sparkling Standard performs a final walkthrough against the signed checklist. The customer should report a missed item promptly.

10.2 Sparkling Standard will review the report and provide reasonable corrective cleaning at no additional charge for verified missed work within the agreed scope.

10.3 The correction procedure is not an unconditional refund promise and does not extend to unrelated work, restoration, repair, or conditions outside the scope.

10.4 A damage claim is handled under Section 21 of the Core Terms.

## 11. Cancellation and Rescheduling

11.1 The cancellation, rescheduling, and failed-access terms in Section 19 of the Core Terms apply.

11.2 A statutory cancellation right is preserved. A fee will not be charged during a statutory cancellation period.

11.3 A cancellation or failed-access fee is not charged automatically from a stored payment method.

## 12. Insurance

Sparkling Standard Cleaning Co. maintains commercial general liability insurance with limits of $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 general aggregate. Evidence of coverage is available upon request. Coverage is subject to the terms, conditions, exclusions, and limits of the applicable insurance policy. The existence of insurance does not expand or reduce either party's contractual rights or responsibilities.
