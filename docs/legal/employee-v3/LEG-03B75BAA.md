# Employee Timekeeping and Location Acknowledgment

**Document ID:** LEG-03B75BAA
**Version:** 2026-11-22.remediation.v1
**Status:** Owner review draft — not effective until approved
**Category:** Employee - Timekeeping

## 1. Accurate Time Records

1.1 The employee will record actual time worked using the approved timekeeping method.

1.2 The employee must clock in when work begins and clock out when work ends. If the employee performs work before or after the recorded time, the employee must record the actual time.

1.3 The employee must not record time for another employee or alter another employee's record.

## 2. Travel Between Jobs

2.1 Travel from one job site to another during the workday is compensable working time when required for the employer's work.

2.2 The employee will use the approved travel action when leaving one job for the next and will not clock out to make work-related travel disappear.

2.3 Ordinary home-to-work and work-to-home commuting is not compensable unless the law or an approved assignment requires payment.

## 3. Breaks and Meals

3.1 A rest break required by law is paid. A bona fide meal break during which the employee is completely relieved of duty is unpaid.

3.2 The employee will record a meal break accurately. If the employee works through the break or remains on duty, the time is compensable.

3.3 The employee should take a 30-minute unpaid meal break when the assignment permits and the employee is relieved of duty.

## 4. Overtime and Corrections

4.1 The employee will record all hours worked. Overtime is paid as required by law, whether or not it was preauthorized.

4.2 The employee will report a missed, incomplete, or incorrect record promptly. Sparkling Standard will correct the record and pay all compensable time.

## 5. Location

5.1 The timekeeping application may capture location when the employee taps a clock or travel action. Location is used for timekeeping, dispatch, routing, safety, and service verification.

5.2 Sparkling Standard does not use the application to track off-duty activity. Location capture should occur only when the employee uses an authorized action.

## 6. Acknowledgment

6.1 The employee acknowledges the timekeeping and location practices in this document and will record actual time worked.
