# Employee Confidentiality and Client Privacy Agreement

**Document ID:** LEG-37752488
**Version:** 2026-11-22.remediation.v1
**Status:** Owner review draft — not effective until approved
**Category:** Employee - Restrictive Covenant

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This Agreement protects Sparkling Standard's legitimate confidential information and trade secrets while preserving employee rights protected by law.

1.2 It is not intended to prevent ordinary competition, the use of general skills, or lawful communication with a government agency.

## 2. Confidential Information

2.1 Confidential information includes nonpublic customer lists and contact records, customer-specific pricing and account information, internal procedures and training materials that are genuinely proprietary, administrative systems and credentials, business plans, confidential financial information, and private marketing or operational information.

2.2 Confidential information does not include information that is public, lawfully received from another source without a confidentiality obligation, independently developed without using confidential information, or general skills and knowledge.

## 3. Employee Obligations

3.1 The employee will use confidential information only for authorized company work.

3.2 The employee will not disclose confidential information to an unauthorized person or use it for a personal business.

3.3 The employee will protect customer information, including names, addresses, access details, schedules, belongings, and private communications.

3.4 The employee will not read, copy, photograph, or remove customer documents or private information except as authorized for the work.

3.5 The employee will report a suspected loss or unauthorized disclosure promptly.

## 4. Protected Activity

4.1 Nothing in this Agreement restricts lawful whistleblowing, communication with a government agency, protected wage or working-condition discussions, protected concerted activity, or another right protected by law.

4.2 The employee may report a concern to a government agency without notice to Sparkling Standard.

4.3 This Section is a protected-activity carve-out and controls over any other provision that could be read to restrict protected activity.

## 5. Duration and Return

5.1 The obligations continue after employment for as long as the information remains confidential or the law protects it.

5.2 The employee will return all company and customer property and information at separation or when requested.

5.3 The employee will not delete information subject to a legal or investigation hold.

## 6. Remedies

6.1 Sparkling Standard may seek an appropriate legal or equitable remedy for a violation, including injunctive relief where the law permits.

6.2 The employee is responsible for a loss caused by the employee's unauthorized disclosure or use.

6.3 This Agreement does not create a liquidated-damages charge, an automatic payment, or a restriction beyond what the law permits.

## 7. Relationship to Other Agreements

7.1 This Agreement is separate from the Employee Handbook, the Nonsolicitation Agreement, and any role-specific Noncompetition Agreement.

7.2 If a provision conflicts with a protected right, the protected right controls.

## 8. Execution

8.1 The employee and Sparkling Standard execute this Agreement. The employee confirms receipt of the Handbook and an opportunity to ask questions.
