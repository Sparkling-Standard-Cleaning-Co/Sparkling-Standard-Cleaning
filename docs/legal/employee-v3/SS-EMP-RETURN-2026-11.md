# Employee Property, Key, Credential and Access Return Agreement

**Document ID:** SS-EMP-RETURN-2026-11
**Version:** 2026-11-22.remediation.v1
**Status:** Owner review draft — not effective until approved
**Category:** Employee - Property

## 1. Company Property

1.1 The employee must return company equipment, uniforms, supplies, documents, keys, fobs, access cards, storage devices, and company records when requested or at separation.

1.2 Equipment remains company property. The employee will not sell, lend, or dispose of it.

## 2. Customer Property

2.1 The employee must return customer keys, access devices, and documents immediately when the authorized purpose ends.

2.2 The company does not retain residential customer house keys. Commercial client keys may be held only under an authorized commercial custody record.

## 3. Credentials

3.1 The employee must not retain or share passwords, access codes, administrator accounts, payment credentials, or customer portal access.

3.2 The owner will revoke system access at separation.

## 4. Data Handling

4.1 The employee will return or delete company and customer information as directed, except information subject to a legal or investigation hold.

4.2 The employee will not copy customer information for personal use.

## 5. Certification

5.1 The employee certifies that all company and customer property in the employee's possession has been returned.

5.2 A missing item must be reported in writing.

## 6. Execution

6.1 The employee and Sparkling Standard execute this Agreement.
