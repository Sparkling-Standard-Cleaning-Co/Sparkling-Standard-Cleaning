# Employee Customer and Employee Nonsolicitation Agreement

**Document ID:** SS-EMP-NONSOLICIT-2026-11
**Version:** 2026-11-22.remediation.v1
**Status:** Owner review draft — not effective until approved
**Category:** Employee - Restrictive Covenant

## 1. Purpose and Legitimate Interest

1.1 This Agreement protects customer relationships and company goodwill that the employee actually receives or learns through confidential information.

1.2 It is not intended to prevent lawful customer choice or ordinary competition.

## 2. Customer Nonsolicitation

2.1 For 24 months after separation, the employee will not use company confidential information or customer relationships to solicit or divert a customer the employee personally served, whose account the employee managed, or about whom the employee received protected confidential information, for competing cleaning services.

2.2 This restriction does not apply to a customer the employee did not serve or learn about through protected information.

2.3 It does not prohibit general advertising, lawful customer choice, or a customer who independently contacts the employee.

## 3. Employee Nonsolicitation

3.1 For 24 months after separation, the employee will not use confidential information to solicit for a competing cleaning business an employee with whom the employee worked directly or learned about through protected information.

3.2 This restriction does not prohibit a lawful communication protected by law.

## 4. Protected Activity

4.1 Nothing in this Agreement restricts lawful whistleblowing, communication with a government agency, protected wage or working-condition discussions, protected concerted activity, or another legally protected right.

## 5. Reasonableness and Remedies

5.1 The restrictions are limited to the legitimate customer-relationship and confidential-information interests described above.

5.2 Sparkling Standard may seek an appropriate legal or equitable remedy for a violation.

5.3 This Agreement does not create a liquidated-damages charge or an automatic payment.

## 6. Execution

6.1 The employee and Sparkling Standard execute this Agreement. The employee confirms an opportunity to ask questions and, where required, to consult counsel.
