# Payroll Direct Deposit Authorization

**Document ID:** LEG-1D7849C8
**Version:** 2026-11-22.remediation.v1
**Status:** Owner review draft — not effective until approved
**Category:** Employee - Payroll

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This form records that the employee completed payroll and direct-deposit setup through the approved payroll provider.

1.2 Sparkling Standard does not collect full bank account numbers on this form.

## 2. Secure Setup

2.1 The employee enters banking information directly in the payroll provider's secure system.

2.2 The company does not accept account numbers by email, text, or this form.

2.3 The employee should confirm the account and routing information in the payroll provider's system before submitting it.

## 3. Verification

3.1 The employee will review the first pay statement and report an error promptly to the owner or payroll provider.

3.2 A payroll correction is handled under the Handbook's pay and payroll correction policy.

## 4. Authorization

4.1 The employee authorizes payroll deposits to the account entered in the approved payroll system.

4.2 The employee may update or cancel the election through the payroll system.

## 5. Acknowledgment

5.1 The employee understands that this form is not a substitute for the payroll provider's secure setup record.
