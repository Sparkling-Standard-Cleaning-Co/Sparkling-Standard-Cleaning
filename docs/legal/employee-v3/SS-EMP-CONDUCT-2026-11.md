# Workplace Conduct and Professional Standards Policy

**Document ID:** SS-EMP-CONDUCT-2026-11
**Version:** 2026-11-22.remediation.v1
**Status:** Owner review draft — not effective until approved
**Category:** Employee - Policy

## 1. Professional Standards

1.1 Sparkling Standard expects employees to work responsibly, treat customers and coworkers professionally, and represent the company appropriately.

1.2 Employees must perform their duties, respect customer property and privacy, follow reasonable workplace instructions, and maintain professional conduct while representing the company.

## 2. Personal Expression

2.1 Employees are free to hold and express personal beliefs, opinions, and viewpoints.

2.2 Ordinary conversation, disagreement, humor, and lawful personal expression are not automatically workplace misconduct simply because someone disagrees or takes offense.

## 3. Prohibited Conduct

3.1 Sparkling Standard does not permit unlawful discrimination, unlawful harassment, threats, violence, retaliation, or conduct that materially interferes with a safe and productive working environment.

3.2 Employment decisions are based on lawful, job-related qualifications, reliability, conduct, performance, and operational needs.

## 4. Reporting and Protected Rights

4.1 Employees may raise a good-faith concern without retaliation, including through an alternative channel when the concern involves the owner or a supervisor.

4.2 Nothing in this policy restricts a right protected by applicable law.
