# Form I-9 Onboarding Procedure (Paper / PDF Workflow)

**Document ID:** SS-EMP-I9-2026-11
**Version:** 2026-11-22.remediation.v1
**Status:** Owner review draft — not effective until approved
**Category:** Employee - Compliance Procedure

## 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1 This procedure governs completion, storage, and retention of the official USCIS Form I-9 for each new employee of Sparkling Standard LLC, doing business as Sparkling Standard Cleaning Co.

1.2 It does not replace the official form, the official instructions, or legal advice. The owner is the proposed I-9 custodian pending final written confirmation.

## 2. Official Form and Current Edition

2.1 Use the official USCIS Form I-9, edition 01/20/25, OMB No. 1615-0047, expiration 05/31/2027, and the official eight-page instructions.

2.2 Verify the current edition at https://www.uscis.gov/i-9 before each use. Do not edit, alter, or substitute the official form.

2.3 The controlled copies are maintained in the private I-9 control folder.

## 3. Timing

3.1 The employee completes and signs Section 1 no later than the first day of employment, and not before accepting the job offer.

3.2 The employer or authorized representative examines the employee's original acceptable documents and completes Section 2 within three business days after the first day of employment. If the employment relationship lasts fewer than three business days, Section 2 is completed on the first day.

3.3 Supplement A is completed by a preparer or translator who assists with Section 1.

3.4 Supplement B is used for reverification, rehire within three years, or a legal name change.

## 4. Document Examination

4.1 The employee chooses which acceptable document or combination to present. The employer must not specify a document or request more or different documents than the form permits.

4.2 The employer examines the original document in the employee's physical presence and records the document title, issuing authority, number, and expiration date.

4.3 A remote or electronic alternative is used only if a compliant process has been approved. The general electronic-signature portal is not a DHS-compliant electronic I-9 system.

## 5. Storage and Access

5.1 Completed forms are stored only in the restricted I-9 records folder, separate from general personnel files.

5.2 Access is limited to the owner and the designated custodian. Access is recorded in the I-9 access log.

5.3 Completed forms are not stored in the website repository, a public cloud link, email, chat, or a marketing folder.

## 6. Retention and Destruction

6.1 Retain the form for three years after the date of hire or one year after employment ends, whichever is later.

6.2 Do not destroy a form during a legal hold, investigation, charge, complaint, or audit.

6.3 Destruction is recorded and uses cross-cut shredding for paper and secure deletion for electronic copies.

## 7. Corrections and Reverification

7.1 A correction is made without obscuring the original entry. The correction is initialed, dated, and explained in an attached memo.

7.2 A missing or incomplete form is completed as soon as practicable. It is not backdated.

7.3 Reverification is completed before the employment authorization expires. The employee chooses the acceptable document.

## 8. Inspections

8.1 An authorized government request is referred to the owner immediately.

8.2 Provide only the requested forms and documents. Record the request, the official's identity, the date, the documents copied, and the response in the access log.

8.3 Do not provide the entire personnel file or disclose I-9 information to a customer or unauthorized employee.
