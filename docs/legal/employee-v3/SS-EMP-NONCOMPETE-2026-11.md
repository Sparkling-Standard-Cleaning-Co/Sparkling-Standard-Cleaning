# Role-Specific Employee Noncompetition Agreement

**Document ID:** SS-EMP-NONCOMPETE-2026-11
**Version:** 2026-11-22.remediation.v1
**Status:** Owner review draft — not effective until approved
**Category:** Employee - Restrictive Covenant

## 1. Status and Role Schedule

1.1 This Agreement is a role-specific noncompetition agreement. It applies only if the employee's completed role schedule identifies a legitimate interest that supports the restriction.

1.2 Ordinary cleaning employees are not automatically subject to a noncompetition restriction.

1.3 The role schedule must identify the employee's role, the specific legitimate interest, the defined territory, the duration, the consideration, and any garden-leave arrangement.

## 2. Legitimate Interests

2.1 A restriction may be supported only by a legitimate interest such as protected customer relationships, confidential pricing or bidding information, proprietary operating methods, or trade secrets.

2.2 The restriction must be no broader than necessary to protect that interest.

## 3. Territory and Duration

3.1 The territory is limited to the branch, ZIP codes, or route where the employee actually worked and had the protected relationship or information.

3.2 The maximum duration is 24 months. A shorter duration applies when the role schedule states one.

3.3 A 24-month term alone does not establish enforceability. The role schedule must support the restriction under applicable law.

3.4 Florida Statutes §542.335 and, where applicable, the CHOICE Act requirements apply. A separate Alabama analysis is required before any Alabama use.

## 4. Consideration and Garden Leave

4.1 The role schedule identifies the consideration for the restriction. Continued employment alone may not be sufficient consideration.

4.2 If the role schedule provides garden leave, the employee remains available to the company during the notice period on the stated terms.

## 5. Protected Activity

5.1 Nothing in this Agreement restricts a legally protected right, including lawful whistleblowing, government communication, protected wage or working-condition discussions, or protected concerted activity.

## 6. Remedies

6.1 Sparkling Standard may seek an appropriate legal or equitable remedy for a violation.

6.2 This Agreement does not create a liquidated-damages charge or an automatic payment.

## 7. Execution

7.1 The employee and Sparkling Standard execute this Agreement and the completed role schedule.
