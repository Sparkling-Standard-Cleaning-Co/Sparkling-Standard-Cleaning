## 1. Timekeeping

I understand that I must:

- clock in and out only for my own work;
- record breaks and meal periods accurately;
- clock in at the assigned job location when required;
- report a missed, incorrect or incomplete time record promptly; and
- never falsify, alter or duplicate a time record.

## 2. Location capture

I understand that:

- the employee portal may capture my location only when I tap a clock-in,
  clock-out or break action;
- the application does not continuously track my location in the background;
- location is used to verify the assigned job and time record, not for unrelated
  surveillance; and
- I can request a correction when a location or time record is inaccurate.

## 3. Corrections

I understand that a correction request must include a reason, and that an
authorized manager will review it. The original entry and the correction are
retained for audit.

## 4. Pay and records

I understand that hours worked must be paid under applicable law, that my pay
rate is stated in my employment records, and that I should report any pay concern
promptly.

## 5. Acknowledgment

I have read this acknowledgment, had an opportunity to ask questions, and agree
to follow it.
