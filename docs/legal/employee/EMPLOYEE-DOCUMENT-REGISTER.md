# Employee Document Register

**Document ID:** SS-EMP-REGISTER  
**Version:** 2026-11-19.v1  
**Status:** Draft - owner and employment-law review required  
**Effective date:** Not effective

This register reconciles the employee documents that exist in the owner master
workspace with the documents that must be completed before work begins. It is a
control document, not legal advice and not an employment contract.

## 1. Mandatory compliance forms (official government forms)

These are official forms. Do not replace them with a company imitation.

| Requirement | Form / record | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Employment eligibility verification | USCIS Form I-9, edition **01/20/25** (OMB 1615-0047, expires 05/31/2027), plus the official eight-page instructions | Controlled copies and the paper/manual procedure are in `05 EMPLOYEES & TRAINING/Employee Policies/I-9/`. Completed forms are restricted to `05 EMPLOYEES & TRAINING/Employee Records/I-9/RESTRICTED-COMPLETED/`. The Sparkling Standard e-signature portal is **not** a compliant electronic I-9 system. |
| Federal income tax withholding | IRS Form W-4 (current edition) | Employee completes |
| Florida new hire reporting | Florida New Hire Reporting Center submission | Owner/payroll must file |
| Employer identification | IRS EIN confirmation | Located in the private formation records |
| Payroll and wage records | Payroll provider records; FL minimum wage compliance | Owner/payroll responsibility |

The company must not request documents that the I-9 process does not permit, and
must not retain copies beyond what the law allows. Employment-law counsel or a
qualified payroll provider should confirm the current rules.

## 4. Required onboarding sequence

1. Signed employment offer or written acceptance.
2. Form I-9 completed within the legal deadline.
3. Form W-4 and payroll records.
4. Florida new hire report.
5. Handbook acknowledgment.
6. Confidentiality and client-privacy acknowledgment.
7. Timekeeping and GPS acknowledgment.
8. Equipment, key and safety briefing.
9. Role qualification and solo/lead approval where applicable.
10. Customer-data access is granted only after the required acknowledgments are
    verified in the admin Documents & Agreements workflow.

## 5. Privacy and access

- Employee personnel records, payroll data, I-9 records and background records
  are confidential and must never be committed to this repository.
- Customer data is accessed only for assigned work and only through authorized
  systems.
- Timekeeping location is captured only when the employee taps a clock action,
  never as continuous tracking.
- A signed generic PDF is not proof of a separate legal process such as I-9
  verification or tax withholding. Those must be completed independently.
