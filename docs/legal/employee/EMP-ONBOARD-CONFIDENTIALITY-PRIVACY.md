## 1. Confidential information

I understand that I may see or handle confidential information, including:

- customer names, contact details, addresses, access codes and schedules;
- customer photographs, notes, agreement records and payment information;
- employee records and internal company information;
- pricing, payroll, financial records and business plans; and
- security procedures, alarm codes and key information.

## 2. My obligations

I will:

- access confidential information only as required for assigned work;
- keep access codes and keys secure and report a loss immediately;
- never post customer addresses, interiors, documents or identifying details on
  social media or send them to anyone outside the company;
- never copy customer information to a personal device, account or storage
  service without written authorization;
- never use customer information for my own benefit or for any purpose unrelated
  to the assigned work;
- report a suspected privacy incident, lost device, lost key or unauthorized
  access immediately; and
- return or securely dispose of confidential information when my work ends or
  when the company asks.

## 3. Customer privacy in the field

I will follow the Photo Privacy SOP, will not photograph identifying documents,
mail, screens, family photographs, prescriptions or valuables, and will not
publish customer photographs without the separate written marketing consent.

## 4. Company privacy

I will not disclose employee or company confidential information except as
required by law or authorized in writing.

## 5. Duration

These obligations continue after my work with the company ends, for as long as
the information remains confidential. They do not restrict any right protected by
law, including lawful whistleblowing or communication with a government agency.

## 6. Acknowledgment

I have read this acknowledgment, had an opportunity to ask questions, and agree
to follow it.
