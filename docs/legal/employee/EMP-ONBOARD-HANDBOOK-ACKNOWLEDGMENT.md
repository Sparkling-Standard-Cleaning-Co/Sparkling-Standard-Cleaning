## 1. Acknowledgment

I confirm that I received and had an opportunity to review the Sparkling
Standard Cleaning Co. Employee Handbook, including the policies on attendance,
timekeeping, safety, customer privacy, equipment, conduct and corrective action.

## 2. Understanding

I understand that:

- the handbook describes company expectations and may be updated in writing;
- I must follow the current policies and ask my supervisor when I am unsure;
- I must protect customer and company private information;
- I must report accidents, injuries, damage, safety hazards and policy concerns
  promptly;
- I must record time accurately and must not clock in for another person;
- I must not access customer property, accounts or information except as
  required for assigned work; and
- company property, keys and equipment must be used only for authorized work and
  returned when requested.

## 3. Not a contract

I understand that this acknowledgment is not an employment contract, does not
promise employment for any period, and does not change any at-will employment
relationship unless a separate written agreement signed by an authorized owner
says so.

## 4. Questions

I had the opportunity to ask questions about the handbook and the policies
covered by this acknowledgment.
