## 5. Approval workflow

A document may become usable only after all of the following are recorded:

1. Owner completes the factual blanks and any owner decisions.
2. Qualified counsel reviews the final wording and records the review.
3. Owner approves the exact version in the admin Documents & Agreements section.
4. The document is uploaded or pasted as a new template and marked approved.
5. The applicable packet is updated and the enforcement stage is chosen.
6. A controlled signing test is completed with synthetic records.
7. The register below records the approval and effective date.

Until then the document stays a draft. A draft never creates a signing
requirement, and the owner must never tell a customer that a document is
attorney-approved without an actual attorney review.

## 7. Flags for counsel

Counsel should specifically review:

- the Florida three-business-day cancellation language and its interaction with
  the recurring-service structure;
- whether any limitation of liability is enforceable and how it should read;
- the commercial Net 15 terms and any late-charge policy;
- the church 10% discount wording and tax-exemption handling;
- the e-signature consent, evidence package and paper-copy procedure;
- the employee acknowledgments and at-will language;
- the operating agreement and member/ownership facts;
- any insurance, bonding or licensing statement before it is published; and
- whether Alabama service needs separate venue/consumer disclosures.
