## 2. What the Operating Agreement must establish

A Florida LLC operating agreement should address, at minimum:

1. The exact legal name and principal address of Sparkling Standard LLC.
2. The member(s), ownership percentages and capital contributions.
3. Management structure: member-managed or manager-managed.
4. Authority to open accounts, sign contracts, hire employees and incur debt.
5. Distributions, tax treatment and allocation of profits and losses.
6. Transfer restrictions, buy-sell terms and admission of new members.
7. Voting thresholds for major decisions.
8. Dissolution and winding-up.
9. Records, notices and meeting requirements.
10. Indemnification and insurance.
11. Governing law and dispute resolution.
12. Whether the company is taxed as a disregarded entity, partnership, S
    corporation or C corporation.

1. Owner completes Section 3.
