## Excluded everywhere (professionally declined)

Post-construction cleaning; biohazards/human waste; severe animal waste; needles/sharps; crime
scenes; severe hoarding; mold remediation; serious infestations incl. bedbugs; hazardous
chemical remediation; fire restoration; unsafe height/ladder work; any work requiring
specialist certification, equipment or insurance the company does not hold.

## Notes

- "Do not promise removal of every stain" applies to all detail work; finishes come first.
- Recurring assumes a reasonably maintainable home; an initial detailed clean may be suggested
  (never forced) when the estimate sees heavy initial work.
- Commercial scopes are written per facility from a walkthrough; this matrix is the baseline
  vocabulary for those scopes, not a substitute for seeing the space.
